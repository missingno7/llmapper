## Did pattern vocabulary improve the prose?

Yes, relative to a sensor dump or "two buildings in a field":

The pattern-aware BB6 prose states that sky *area* and sky *sector count*
disagree; that starts are already in hunting space rather than closets; that
1-exit pads can still belong to the field; that all-sky geodesics do not
imply missing interiors; that 2D spawn peek is not concealment; and that
fortresses are holed outdoor hosts plus off-geodesic cover.

Those relations are reconstructable. Coordinates are not in the prose.

## Did it improve reconstruction?

Partially.

**Better than a naive reading:** twin masses, mixed spawn types, sky
porches with large local area, all-sky routes, gated flags, a central
depression, 8/8 DM connectivity.

**Not yet deeper understanding:** fortress-scale interiors, irregular
masonry, mechanism chains, BB6 material/shade, vertical overlooks. The
candidate is still a diagram of relations, not Blood architectural language.

That is the same class of failure the BB2 roundtrip exposed, now measured
on a second original map.

## Compression note

Removing pairwise-2D-open as a reconstruction target *improved* honesty:
optimizing it would have fought the masses. Removing "rectangle count"
would not have hurt — both maps have rectangles, and they do not carry
design. Removing sky-area vs sky-sector *would* hurt: it is why interiors
must grow in a later pass.
