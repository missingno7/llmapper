# Reasoned authoring v1: cliffside monastery

## Brief

An abandoned cliffside monastery containing an arrival garden courtyard, an
embedded chapel, a lower crypt, and an upper service gallery. The areas must have
distinct spatial and visual identities while forming one coherent explorable
place.

This theme is a benchmark brief for the authoring loop, not a level primitive.
Nothing in `bloodmap` knows what a monastery is.

## What this experiment is actually testing

Whether an LLM produces a better and more complex Blood level when it receives an
independent, structured explanation of what its generated level *became*, rather
than only a confirmation that the MAP is valid.

The test is not "does the level pass". Every iteration passed every hard gate
from the first blockout onward. The test is whether the independent evidence
changed the design decisions, and whether the resulting changes are visible in
the evidence and in the rendered frames.

## Required spatial complexity

* several major assemblies rather than one chain of rectangular rooms
* a large exterior parent space
* at least one embedded building or shell
* multiple interior spaces
* at least two meaningful vertical transitions
* one spatial loop or revisit
* one optional side space or secret candidate
* one intentionally composed constrained-to-open transition
* one large space with internal children and details
* a readable start and exit
* decoration attached compositionally to parent structures

Satisfying these by sector proliferation is explicitly not acceptable; the
hierarchy has to reflect actual spatial composition.

## Mechanism scope

Deliberately modest: one direct-use door, one remote-gated door, one skull-key
gate, a normal exit, and one optional secret. Combat, enemy behaviour, and causal
mechanism reconstruction are out of scope, and nothing in this project measures
them.

## Rules the experiment holds itself to

* An authored label is intent. `role="courtyard"` never answers a question about
  what the compiled space is.
* No single quality score. Structure, space, progression, material, scale, shape,
  and uncertainty stay separate.
* A skipped evaluator is never a pass.
* Original Blood maps are precedents for principles, never sources of copied
  coordinates or fragments.
* Generated candidates are never evidence about original Blood design patterns.
* Every iteration is preserved as source, MAP, evidence packet, rendered views,
  and review. The final result does not overwrite the record that produced it.
