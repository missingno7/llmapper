# Blind review: two unlabelled builds, eleven views

One fresh reviewer (Opus). M = after, R = before (the build the owner
walked). Not told which was which or what changed. Interpretation, not
measurement. Condensed; quotations are its words.

"M is clearly better."

## Better in the new build

- `pawn_west`: "R stacks lettering on top of art." M: "clearly more
  believable".
- `unit_e`: "M's shelf behind the BOOKS sign is full of books ...
  decisively more believable. A book shop whose shelf shows urns is
  illogical."
- `unit_d`: before, "statues ... crammed into the shelf, with their heads
  touching the top board".
- `unit_a`: before, "shrubs on a shop shelf and on a counter read as
  overgrowth, not goods".
- `yard`: before, "the hanging lantern's chain goes up to nothing".
- Lamps: "M's lamp size reads more real"; before, "shorter than doors and
  ground-floor windows: miniature".

## Better in the old build

- `avenue`: a near lamp "gives the avenue scale and a foreground"; the new
  build has none near the camera.
- `pawn_window`: "a statue on a plinth is plausible pawn-shop window stock.
  M's lone wine glass is odd." (The owner had said the opposite of statues;
  the owner's word stands.)

## Still wrong in the new build

1. "The half-body torso sunk into the book-shop floor", "the stray dynamite
   and floor skull".
2. "The lamps stand anywhere in the plaza, one in front of a door, instead
   of along a kerb. The streets have no regular lamp rhythm."
3. "Chairs clipping into tables, a chair covering a picture, and bottles the
   size of chair backs."

## What was done with it

The lamp by the door: no post now stands within 1,536 of a door, slot or
pool. The two chairs between the tables are gone. Not reviewed again.

Not done: the torso is an enemy (type 203) whose map picture is its
rising-from-the-ground frame; the game replaces it, the observer does not.
I have not seen it in the game. The dynamite is a pickup. The plaza lamps
are light-pool lamps with no kerb near them. Bottle sizes are the town
maps' own repeats.
