# Blind review: three unlabelled versions of the saloon and the arcade units

One fresh reviewer (Opus), three folders of the same ten views and nothing
else: K = full, Q = before, T = full with every thing on a shelf removed.
Not told which was which or what had changed. Interpretation, not
measurement. Condensed; quotations are its words.

## What it saw

- Before: "low brick- or wood-sided blocks with flat grey slab tops ... The
  back walls of the shops are bare." The saloon "reads more like a cellar
  with crates"; the units "like a display hall or mortuary".
- Full: "a long wooden counter with a slatted shelf unit behind it"; in the
  saloon "a long ... wooden bar ... lined with bottles", and of the liquor
  shelf under WHISKEY, "exactly right".
- Bare: "the same recess, empty. It is a dark slatted hole ... looks
  unfinished."

## Ranking

Full over bare over before in `unit_b`, `unit_b_rake`, `unit_c`, `unit_f`.
Full = bare over before in `saloon_in`, `saloon_counter` (identical frames).
`saloon_shelf`: full, before, bare. `unit_e`: before >= full > bare. The two
controls identical.

## How much each thing matters

- "Back-bar or back shelf stocked with goods ... decisive. It is the single
  thing that turns these boxes from 'room with a counter' into a specific
  trade."
- "Long wooden counters vs grey-topped blocks: decisive."
- "Counter dressing, many items vs a few: helps."

## Faults it found in the full set

1. "A bald scalp sticks up out of the middle of the book counter." A zombie,
   shut behind a counter that could no longer be stepped over.
2. "The bookshop has no books ... candles, urns and a statuette instead."
3. "The four shops are one kit": the same box, floor, light, counter and
   shelf; "only the sign colour and the small items change".
4. "The same fanged-demon painting hangs in the saloon, the apothecary and
   the bookshop."
5. "The counter tops show the wall plank texture laid flat ... streaky."
6. "The saloon tables are solid ... wooden boxes. They have no legs and
   there are no chairs or stools anywhere."
7. "Each shelf is one straight row of identical-height items, which reads as
   placed, not stocked."
8. "Game pickups are used as stock: the dynamite ... and the key."
9. "The ceiling is a modern grey suspended-tile grid in a 1920s wooden
   saloon."

## Most valuable still missing

"Each trade needs its own stock and furniture, not a re-labelled shell ...
The sign is currently doing all the work."

## What was done with it

(1) was worse than it looked: a counter 8,192 high, run wall to wall, shut
the arcade's KEY, a pickup and the zombie behind it. Every counter now stops
a player width short of one wall, the finds stand on the customer's side,
and a test holds both. The final build was not shown to a reviewer. (2) to
(9) stand as found.
