# Blind review, round 2 (low eaves, buttresses, glass, 1.9-body gables)

Reviewer: a fresh model instance, no memory of round 1, given only the twelve
frames in `round2/frames/` under the same instructions. One reviewer, one
pass: an interpretation, not a measurement.

## What it said the grey building is

- avenue_south: "church/chapel (likely)".
- avenue_front: "Church/chapel front (likely, purely from the coloured
  glass)". No gable, pediment, steps, rose window, porch or roof; "the top is
  a flat crenellated line, so it reads as a fortress or warehouse".
- portal: "a gate into a walled compound (likely)... looks like a garage
  door".
- avenue_south_up: "Steeple/bell tower (guess)... no spire, no belfry
  openings and no cap, so it reads as a chimney or pillar".
- vista_far: "the best frame. The tower reads as an obelisk, not a steeple".
- cemetery_side: "nothing here says cemetery".

## Its three largest faults

1. Black voids at every street end and the black silhouette in spawn_north.
2. The church has no ecclesiastical silhouette: flat top, no roof or gable,
   a tower without a spire; "the skybox's painted spires upstage it".
3. Huge featureless monotextured facades elsewhere in the city, no doors at
   street level, no props, no lighting variation.

## Against round 1

| | round 1 | round 2 |
|---|---|---|
| the building | warehouse / fortress (guess) | church / chapel (likely) |
| the cue named | none | the coloured glass |
| the tower | factory chimney (likely) | chimney / obelisk; steeple (guess) |
| the door | shed or service door | garage door |

The reading of the building moved from guess to likely and to the right
answer. The tower and the door did not move. Round 3 steepened the gables
(1.9 to 3.9 bodies, ridge 17.4) and raised the portal (1.9 to 2.4 bodies).
Round 3's own blind review is `critic-round3.md`.

## A thing only the reviewer saw

The sky tile itself paints spires on the horizon, and they compete with the
tower. That is a property of tile 3491 in this city and nobody had written
it down.
