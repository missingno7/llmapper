# Blind review, round 1 (massing only)

Reviewer: a separate model instance given only the eleven street frames in
`round1/frames/`. It was not told what the level is, what any building is
meant to be, or that anything had changed, and was told not to read anything
else in the repository. One reviewer, one pass: an interpretation, not a
measurement.

## What it said the grey building is

- avenue_south: "civic or warehouse block (guess)"
- avenue_south_up: steps up into "a taller plain grey shaft, which could be a
  tower (guess)". No openings, no belfry, no crown.
- avenue_front: "Warehouse or fortress (guess)". Door tiny against the wall,
  no frame or steps.
- avenue_front_up: "a blank box".
- portal: "a shed or service door (likely)".
- vista_far: "reads as a factory chimney (likely), not as a church".

## Its three largest faults

1. The grey building is a blank, banded box: no windows, openings or
   roofline, nothing in its shape says what it is; the tower is an untextured
   shaft without a top.
2. The main door is undersized and unframed.
3. Black voids end every vista, and there are paper-thin free-standing
   slabs.

## What it listed as missing for a church

Pointed or arched openings, buttresses, a rose window, a gable, a belfry with
louvres, a spire on the tower itself, steps, a recessed arched portal,
stained glass, a cross.

## What was done with it

Round 2 took the first fault: lowered the eaves, added buttresses, lancets
and gabled tower faces. Fault 2 was taken in round 3. Fault 3 is the city's
edge and is not this experiment; it is recorded in
`reports/visual-survey-2026-11-17.md`.
