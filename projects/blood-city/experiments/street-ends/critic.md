# Blind review, street ends against before

One fresh reviewer (Opus), two unlabelled folders of the nine `vistas`
frames (A = end walls built, faces not yet lit; B = before) and nothing
else. Interpretation, not measurement. Condensed; quotations are its words.

A in 9 of 9.

| view | A | B |

| avenue_north_mid | "two facades side by side, grey stone and brown brick, both with windows, plus red sky above (certain)" | "one flat grey brick-pattern wall that fills the frame to the top, with no sky" |

**Slab, void, edge of the world.** B: "yes ... each is a tall, pure-black,
flat-topped rectangle cut into the sky. It reads as a void or an unlit box."
A: "the same spots are dark, but they are low enough to read as distant,
unlit buildings under the sky. They are not good, but they are not slabs."
Both: `market_west_far` "has a black rectangle at the left edge".

**City or set wall.** A: "the ends read as buildings across the street";
where two materials meet, "two adjacent buildings, not an error". B: "one
repeated grey brick-scar tile, flat, with no openings, base or top. This is
a backdrop or a set wall."

**Faults.** B only: "two thin light horizontal lines float across the end
wall" (`avenue_north_mid`), "a thin diagonal light line" (`market_east_near`).
A: the dark block's top edge "ruler-straight against the sky ... a mild
'cardboard' look". "I saw no sky bleeding through solid surfaces."

**What still weakens A.** (1) "Far views: the end is still an untextured
dark block ... the street dies into black, not into a lit facade." (2) "The
end facades sit on grey brick filler walls ... the facades look pasted onto
them." (3) "No door at street level, no sidewalk return and no depth change
where the street meets them, so the street just stops against a picture of a
building."
