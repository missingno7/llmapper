# Blind review: five unlabelled versions of the pawn shop's interior

One fresh reviewer (Opus), five folders of the same four views and nothing
else: A = empty, B = full, C = before, D = unlit, E = nobars. Not told which
was which. Interpretation, not measurement. Condensed; quotations are its
words.

## What is in the back wall

- **C (before):** "There is nothing in the back wall ... I am certain this
  is a pawnshop because of the sign" (LOANS).
- **A (empty):** "an empty barred niche over a patterned ledge ... Empty, it
  reads more like a jail cell (guess)."
- **B (full):** "likely a locked cage of pledged or high-value goods behind
  a barred counter."
- **D (unlit):** "Identical to B, but the inside of the cage is darker."
- **E (nobars):** "an open recessed display closet ... It reads as a
  clothing boutique, and the pawn or security read is gone."

## Ranking

`pawn_to_back`: B = D > E > A > C. `pawn_back_rake`: E > B = D > A > C.
`pawn_back_close`: E > D > B > A >> C. `pawn_length`: C > A = B = D = E
("only C identifies the shop: the LOANS sign").

## What differs, and how much it matters

- "A vs B: goods inside the cage. Decisive. It turns a jail cell into a
  shop's secure stock."
- "B vs D: lighting inside the cage only ... barely noticeable."
- Bars removed: "helps depth and legibility, and hurts the pawn-cage
  identity."
- Best overall: D, "E is a close second".

## Still wrong

1. "Shop identity and the cage are never seen together. Only C has the LOANS
   sign, and C has no cage."
2. "The barred niche is unreachable and has no purpose. No counter or clerk
   position meets it ... It reads as wall decoration."
3. "The room is ONE flat box. It has a single uniformly lit ceiling, flat
   even light everywhere, no level change, no shadowed corner, no light
   source and no pool of light. The only depth is the 1 m niche."

## Faults it listed

The left dark-wood panel "visibly stretched vertically" and the niche's
stone jambs "stretched and smeared" (all sets); "a thin pale stick sprite
floats on the counter top" in `pawn_length`; the orange coat showing "only as
a sliver" at a rake; `pawn_back_close` unusable in C, where the camera faces
a wall.
