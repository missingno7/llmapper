# Blind review: four unlabelled versions of the dressed town

One fresh reviewer (Opus), four folders of the same ten views and nothing
else: W = full without its new objects, X = full, Y = before, Z = full
without its new signs, bills and notices. Interpretation, not measurement.
Condensed; quotations are its words.

"The main split is objects ... In practice X is roughly Z, and W is roughly
Y." Rankings: X = Z > W = Y in eight views; all equal in `foyer_in`;
X > Z > W > Y in `saloon_door`.

## What each place is

- `saloon_counter`, bare: "wooden room with bench-like blocks and fires on
  top. Could be a sauna, a forge or a crypt. Guess." Dressed: "bar or
  saloon, likely. Evidence: objects (wine glasses, a bottle, a painting)."
- `saloon_in`, bare: "shrine or crematorium" (guess). Dressed: "tavern,
  likely."
- `shopfront`: apothecary, likely, from lettering; with "a red potion and a
  jar on the counters ... certain".
- `nave`, bare: "church or crypt, likely". Dressed: "church nave, certain.
  Evidence: objects (altar candles, a banner, a statue)."
- `pawn_front`, `super_east`, `foyer_in`: certain in all four, from
  lettering.
- `saloon_door`, `hall_face`: a guess in all four. "The bucket says
  'danger', not 'hall'."

## How much each kind of thing matters

"Props on counters and tables: decisive." "Altar dressing: decisive for
church versus crypt." "Wall art: helps." "Shop goods behind glass: helps."
"Gore buckets and piles: barely helps for mood, and hurts for identity."
"Trees and shrubs: barely." "The extra sign fragment and poster: barely."
"Where a view has no lettering (saloon, hall, door), only objects can carry
identity."

## Placement problems in the best set

- "Open fires burn on top of the tables ... Burning bar tables are absurd."
- "The same gore bucket or pile is placed at the foot of three different
  doorways. It is the most obvious repeat."
- "The torch overlaps the bottom of the green banner."
- "The statue on its plinth is crammed behind a mannequin."
- "The goods are tiny lumps scattered on the floor rather than on shelves."
- Two figures "sunk" in the floor (a monster rising, and one behind a fire).

## Kit or town

"It is a small kit ... Interiors get 1 to 3 props each. The streets get only
gore."

## Most valuable still missing

1. "No bar counter, shelves or barrels in the saloon, and fire on the
   tables."
2. "Shops have no fixtures ... shelves and racks with stock on them."
3. "Facades without lettering or a hanging sign are anonymous."

## What was done with it

The bins (tile 505) and the bar's flames were removed; the statue and the
banner were moved. The rest stands as the next work. The final build has not
been shown to a reviewer.
