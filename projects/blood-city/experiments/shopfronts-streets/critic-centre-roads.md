# Blind review, centred roads against before

One fresh reviewer (Opus), two unlabelled folders of the same 17 views (Q =
the build with roads down the middle of the seam streets and a paved crossing
left at every junction; M = before) and nothing else. Interpretation, not
measurement. Condensed; quotations are its words.

Per view: Q in 15, M in 1 (`hall_band`, "slightly"), neither in 1
(`foundry_road`). `kerb_along`: "a clean central road, kerbs on both sides
and lamps ... the best street frame". `pawn_rake`: in M "the mannequins are a
texture on the wall", in Q "they stand in a real recessed display. Q, by a
lot."

**Streets.** M: "wall-to-wall cobbles, with no kerbs and no walking/driving
split." Q: "yes, mostly. There is a road in the middle and slab pavements
with kerbs."

**Junctions.** M: "one undifferentiated cobble field ... it reads as
nothing." Q: "the cross street's pavement runs unbroken across the mouth of
the avenue, with a kerb. The avenue's road ends at a kerb. That reads as an
error: carts would hit a kerb."

**Other things in Q that look like mistakes.** `avenue_south`: "the road is
off to the right: the left pavement is about twice as wide as the right
one". `kerb_close`: "the pavement runs straight on past the building's
corner instead of turning with it". `spawn_wide`: "a loose patch of cobbles
at the far right ... looks stray".

**Textures.** `mall_north` Q: "the frieze above the left shop is cut by a
vertical plaster strip". `hall_band` M: "a crate X-lattice texture is used as
a wall band"; Q: "isolated red brick panels of different heights over each
opening, with grey plaster showing between them ... patchy and unfinished.
Neither is right; M at least reads as one deliberate band."
`market_road_level` Q: "the platform trim is a bright concrete that clashes".

**Too dark in both.** "the vistas at the ends of streets ... black void below
the skyline".

**Top three still wrong in Q.** (1) "Junctions block the road." (2) "Road
placement is inconsistent." (3) "Trim is broken or patchy."

## What was done with it

(1) Each run now reaches a junction's depth past its end nodes and roads
meet edge to edge: the avenue opens onto the market street and onto Theatre
Row. The "stray" cobbles in `spawn_wide` and the pavement that "ignores the
corner" were both the same paved crossing and went with it. (2) The avenue's
road beside the tower is still east of centre: the tower stands in the
street. (3) Not done. The final build has not been shown to a reviewer.
