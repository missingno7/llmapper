# The pump station's machine hall: what is meant

The gameplay plan makes the flame key the hall's reason; the hall was built before
there was a key, and from its door a player saw a drum 1.5 units away and nothing else.
Built by `level/l3_shed.py`; studied in `knowledge/blood/studies/machine-hall/notes.md`.

1. **The key is seen before it is reached.** The gallery climbs a body from the lobby and
   comes out on a railed deck over the floor (E3M6 shows its moon key so, up between two drums). From
   there, over the rail: the belt, the two presses, and the key lit between them.
2. **The key has its own light.** The middle stretch of the belt is the widest and lies under a hood
   whose underside is a strip light (2288). It is the brightest thing in the hall.
3. **The lever is the second thing seen.** The console bank is lit straight ahead of the deck, and the lever
   beside it stands between hazard strips under its own lamp, lettered BELT. The way to it is clear.
4. **Down and up** by three treads at the deck's west end, between the basin and the belt.
5. **The presses never stop**, the belt does: take the key with the belt running and it carries you under one.
6. **Three come when it is taken**, each from a place: out of the sewage in the settling basin; on the deck, between you and
   the way out; out of the dark south bay.
7. **Simplified.** Gone: the filter drum (52 walls), four strip lights in a roof six bodies up that lit nothing, the basin's
   riser, a dozen pilasters. The south bay is empty on purpose.
8. One process is still readable: INTAKE over the basin, SLUDGE along the belt's line, the consoles.
9. **One machine says what the hall does** (2026-11-25, `level/pump_engine.py`): the filter hangs over the basin, a round lamp in its mesh, a pipe from
   it to the consoles and one to the belt, and a pump stands either side of it whose rod never stops. The drum of 7 is not back: this one is the
   far side of what is seen from the deck. The lamps are tubes: they were 2183, which is a trap that fires out of a wall.
