# Gravesend Station: what is meant

The owner, 2026-11-25: "That station side entry probably doesn't make sense any
more, it was the original entry before we made that main one, I would also rework
the station itself a bit, there are some old process things like at the end location
badly aligned box textures. You can look for stations references like E1M2 for
example." Before that, of the front: "makes buildings next to it to look like paper
thin because of the sky", "maybe we can add some columns", and "the buildings on the
sides of station have windows cut off, maybe we can just use that side without windows
variants of textures there".

Built by `level/l3_station.py` (the front, the platform, the trains) and
`level/booking_hall.py`. Studies: `knowledge/blood/studies/station`, `locomotive`.

1. **One way in, the avenue's.** A forecourt under the street's own sky, its sides the
   blank sides of the two buildings it stands between; a portico of four columns; the
   town's name and the clock over it. Two arches, shut by roll-up gates until the six
   keys are turned on the pier between them. The door in the north lane, its passage
   and its ramp are gone; a hearth stands where the ramp came up.
2. **The booking hall** (E1M2 s92 for its height, its checker floor, its long double
   benches and its business along the sides): on the portico's axis, the benches and a
   flight of stairs, the clock over it. An iron roof rises from the side walls to a strip
   of glass on two rows of riveted columns. West, three ticket windows and the office
   behind them (its way in is open); east, the refreshment counter (it can be climbed)
   and telephones. Nothing else.
3. **One axis.** Up the flight is the waiting hall, as wide as the booking hall and open
   to it across the whole end; through the middle of its three open bays, between the
   riveted columns, the platform and the carriage. West of it the luggage window (the
   first booking office), east the waiting room with its hearth and its longcase clock.
   ("The general rooms placement is still leftover from that original main enterance on
   the side ... that room on the side looks like something that should be part of main
   enterance": all the old station was moved by `l3_station.MOVE` to stand behind it.) The
   turnstile is gone: "little weird if you can just go around it". What stands is built:
   the clock and the telephones are blocks, as Blood's are.
4. **The end is the train.** The signal box's lever (up the stair at the platform's east
   end) raises the gate in the tunnel's mouth: a whistle, the ground shakes, and what was in
   the tunnel comes up the platform. Six seconds later the carriage's door, before the
   waiting hall, sinks. The emblem that ends the level is at the carriage's forward end,
   toward the engine. (It hung in a tunnel, and then in a goods store of crates past it.)
5. **Past the tunnel's gate** the line goes on into the dark. Nothing is there.
