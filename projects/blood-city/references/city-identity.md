# Gravesend — the city's identity card

Every material, light, and layout choice downstream must be defensible from
this page.

**Name**: Gravesend. A river-port city of the Blood era — brick, iron,
coal-smoke, and gaslight.

**Hour**: 3 a.m., a dead clear night. No fog (DWE3M1's fog stays in Death
Wish). Sky dark; street floors hold the night-town shade band the sources
agree on (≈+32 floor shade, E3M1 norm), and every lit thing — marquee, window,
brazier — is a *pool* against that band, never a wash.

**Why this place exists, and what happened**: Gravesend grew fat where the
rail line meets the river — stockyards feeding the slaughter-houses, coal
feeding the foundry, and a theatre street feeding the men who ran both. The
Cabal did not need to break in; it bought a box at the opera. On the night of
the last performance the doors of the Aldermack were chained from the inside,
and what walked out when they opened again was not the audience. The city has
been "under quarantine" for three weeks. The gas is still lit because nobody
is left at the works to shut it off. Caleb comes in the way freight does — off
the river, at the bottom of the town — because the Aldermack's curtain is
about to rise on something that should stay in the pit.

**Landmark**: the **Aldermack Opera House**, anchor of Theatre Row and the
main circuit's objective. Silhouette: a stepped fly-tower rising 6–8 standing
heights over a 2-story street wall (skyline contract: area-weighted median
2–4k xy, landmark 6–8 standing), with a lit vertical sign on the tower face —
readable down the length of the main avenue from the river gate (Phase 1
sight-line requirement).

**Districts** (four, per the ≈700–1100 walls-per-district norm):

1. **Market Slip** — the river gate and market hall; the city's front door
   and its plaza. Cobble floors, canvas awnings, the widest spaces. *Carries
   the start and the plaza contract.*
2. **Theatre Row** — gas-lit entertainment street: the Aldermack (landmark
   complex), a saloon, a shooting-parlor attraction, an open-front pawn
   shop. Marquee flicker at every mouth. *Carries the venue cluster and the
   destruction set-piece reserve.*
3. **Old Crossing** — the pre-boom quarter the city grew around: timber over
   brick, narrow alleys, laundry lines, party-wall roofs. *Carries the
   E3M1-style stacked roof route, and is the hand-shaped exception district
   (anti-blandness clause).*
4. **Foundry Ward** — coal yards, the works, the rail spur; the darkest
   district, lit by furnace-glow accents. *Carries the sewer network beneath
   its streets (true ROR, per the sewer contract) with one drop entry in an
   alley and one interior stair entry inside the works.*

**Register notes**: Blood's Americana, not Duke's — no neon-pop, no humor
signage; hand-painted boards, iron brackets, gas globes. Palette identities
per district: Market Slip cobble/canvas, Theatre Row warm gaslight against
deep shadow, Old Crossing timber/plaster, Foundry Ward soot brick and ember
light. District seams fall at street junctions (E3M1 seam norm), never
mid-block.
