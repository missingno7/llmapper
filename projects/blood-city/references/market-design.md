# The market, the stalls and the Demeter

The owner, 2026-11-25, after playing: "In front of provisions there are just some

Modules: `level/meat_market.py` (the hall and the stalls), `level/harbour_ship.py`
(the ship), called from `level/l3_market.py`. Studies: `knowledge/blood/studies/
meat-market`, `harbour`.

## Intent

1. The three platforms go. Two stalls stand at the plaza's west corners, a butcher's
   to the south and a grocer's to the north, with the hall's door clear between
   them. Each is one counter sector (8 walls); the roof, valance, poles and boards
   are sprites. Striped, because it is the only colour on the pavement.
2. The hall is one picture: two files of five carcasses, hung to head height, from
   the street door to a shut steel door on the same axis. Between and around them
   the chequer is bare but for blood.
3. The hall is one act: the clerk's gallery (1.45 bodies over the floor, its front a
   wall of jar shelves) has the lever that raises the steel door. It is reached by a
   stair from the porters' lobby at the back door; the clerk shoots from it.
4. The cold store, a step up, 4 by 8.5 m, white walls frosted at the foot: two
   butchers shut in with the meat, two men among the carcasses, the armor at the end
   that cannot be seen from the door. Taking it brings two dead down and a butcher
   in behind.
5. The ship is the Phantom's size (13 by 4 units), called DEMETER, and can be
   boarded: a gangplank through the gunwale, beside the deckhouse. Nothing on deck
   but a mast, two crates and a lantern.
6. Her hold is the deckhouse's inside, three steps down, roofed with flat sprites:
   two of the Cabal among barrels, the flare pistol.
7. A panel of other planks in the hold's stern sinks when pushed: the smugglers'
   locker, a secret: dynamite, which opens a manhole (a shorter way to the sewers)
   long before any other is found.
8. Taking the flare pistol brings three gill beasts up onto the deck, between the
   player and the gangplank.
9. No ceiling but the sky's aboard or over a stall: a roofed sector under a sky
   eleven bodies up draws a wall to its top.
10. The jetty keeps two boxes of flares and the view; a post in four went.
