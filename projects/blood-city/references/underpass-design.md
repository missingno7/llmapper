# The underpass: what is meant

The owner, 2026-11-24, after playing it: "I would also rework the underpass, add
there some posters and things, making it look more liminal." Built by
`level/underpass.py`; studied in `knowledge/blood/studies/underpass/notes.md`.

1. **Long.** It joins the works yard (mouth at 49, 18..20) to the avenue twelve units
   further south (mouth at 39, 31..33). Between them one passage, two units wide and
   1.7 bodies high, thirteen units from end wall to end wall: 22 metres, with a blind
   corner at each end. From neither street can the other be seen.
2. **Repetitive.** Six beams, one bay between each two, and on the west wall of every
   bay the same bill at the same height. In the last bay it is another bill.
3. **Lit from a few tubes.** One tube to a bay, all alike. Two are dead and one is
   failing (`underpass.LOOSE`); the light goes with the lamp (`light_stage.LampGroup`).
   Each bay is one sector and one light: no pool is cut, and a bay is as dark as its tube.
4. **Nearly empty.** Nobody walks here. Four pieces of paper on the floor, no more.
5. **Finished like a public place that has been let go.** DWE2M2's grey plaster over a
   red tiled dado; on the stairs the grimy tiles, and underfoot the interlocking paving, of the
   owner's own metro (DNE3L6). The finish stops at the gate: past it is bare brick and concrete.
6. **The shut ticket hall.** A wide low room half way, its tube dead: a ticket window behind
   its roll-up shutter, the prices, a clock, notices over a bench, the department's board about the track.
7. **The metro behind its gate.** A folding lattice, floor to ceiling, and behind it the bed
   of a line that was begun. Far down it one bulb.
8. **One thing is wrong: a pram under the bulb.** The only scare: the first time the player
   walks OUT of the ticket hall, the bulb goes out and the pram comes down to the gate, behind him
   (`underpass:pram`; AMB19, a squeaky wheel, is its voice). Nothing attacks. Whoever never
   goes in never sets it off.
9. **At the end of the long view a lit case**: COMING SOON, BLOOD, THE MOVIE.
10. **From the street it is an underpass**: its name over both mouths, a mouth one storey
    of the facade high (so it takes the place of whole painted windows), the steps at once, a rail down
    their middle, and the lit passage at their foot.

What a player gets for going in: the spray can and the gasoline, in the ticket hall; the freight
alcove's shells, off the second bay; and a way between the yard and the avenue no street offers.

Walls: the passage that was here and the one that is cost about the same (the light stage used to
cut the old one into bands; nothing cuts this one).
