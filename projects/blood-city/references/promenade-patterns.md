# Promenade patterns — DWE3M10, the Market Slip supplement

Owner-confirmed source (2026-08-27 screening): a promenade along a pier.
Measured through the improved classifier (city-norms-v2.json, per_map
DWE3M10; plot [plots/dwe3m10-city-plan.png](plots/dwe3m10-city-plan.png)).
This is the direct precedent for Market Slip's river gate.

## Measured anatomy

- **One linear band**: 59 street sectors in a single ~60k-unit north-south
  run; 1 walk-around loop (linear waterfronts don't loop — the loop contract
  is inland urbanism's).
- **Quay width**: median 5632, p10 1792 (gangways and stair necks), p90
  16000 (the plaza bulge mid-promenade) — a street-class walk that widens
  into squares rather than holding one width.
- **Single-sided frontage with plaza exceptions**: doorway facing census
  23 landward-west vs 14 east, 16 north/south — the buildings line the land
  side; the east/cross doors cluster in the mid-promenade bulge.
- **Doorway rate along the water: 1.76/10240** with 6 substantial interiors
  (0.2/10240 — inside the city band). A waterfront is *denser* in doors per
  frontage than a DukCity street (0.23–0.61) because all its frontage is on
  one side.
- **The far side is water as sectors, not backdrop**: of the east-facing
  boundary, 215k units run as red walls into water sectors against only
  12.5k of white wall — the water is real, swimmable space, with boats
  built as sector geometry standing in it (visible in the plot). The
  "backdrop" treatment is reserved for the far shore (50 scene components,
  mostly the distant landscape shelf).

## Market Slip contract (supplement to the city contracts)

- The quay keeps its `row` width (6144) but earns one **bulge** — the plaza
  already planned — rather than uniform width; necks down to lane width at
  the map edge are attested (p10 1792).
- Frontage is **single-sided**: venues (`ferry_office`, the market front)
  face the water from the land side; the water side carries no doors.
- The river beyond the quay becomes **water sectors with at least one boat
  as geometry**, not a painted edge: Phase 2 massing adds a water strip
  south of the quay; the E2M1-style backdrop treatment applies only beyond
  it (far shore silhouette, see backdrop-and-weave.md).
- Doorway rate target on the quay: ~1.5–2.0/10240 of quay frontage —
  denser than the inland streets, per the source.
