# The Gravesend Arcade: intent

Built by `level/arcade.py` (the hall) and `level/l3_mall.py` (street door, service passage, secret); studied in
`knowledge/blood/studies/arcade/notes.md`. Plan 39 to 53 by 40 to 50; the street stands at 8,192.

1. **One section, not one hall.** The street comes in on a low papered gallery; beyond its balustrade the arcade drops a
   body and a half to a paved court and rises to a lantern with the red sky in it.
2. **The axis**: street door, escalator head, dead fountain under the lantern, landing, eye key on its plinth, service
   door, clock. All of it is seen from the door; none of it can be walked straight to.
3. **The gallery is broken.** Its middle span has fallen: one end still hangs from the landing, the other lies in the
   court. Down the stopped escalator, past the basin, up the fallen span.
4. **Three shops, none like another.** Pickman's Rare Books on the gallery: lit, a reader at his desk. Miguel's Guns and
   Ammo on the gallery: shut. The draper's in the court: one window smashed, one still lit with two mannequins and a
   SALE board, and in the dark behind them more. Ichabod's, on the landing, is a gate in the wall.
5. **Negative space**: the court's floor is empty but for the basin and one gas lamp, which stands where the way up is.
6. **The fight.** Taking the key raises Miguel's shutter (chain lift): two come out of the gun shop on the gallery
   opposite, one stands between you and the street, one gets up in the draper's and comes by the span. You are on the
   landing with two marble posts, the plinth, and behind you the door the key opens, with the tommy gun in it.
7. **The reward of the fight** is the gun shop, open: shells and a box on the counter's side.
8. **The secret** is unchanged: the fire extinguisher in the service passage, and the cache behind it.
9. **Overlap**: the court lies west of x 48.5, clear of the Stockyard Drain under the landing (x 48.75 and on). The
   landing and what stands at the street's level are in the street's layer; the court has its own (`arcade.LAYER`).
10. **Light**: the sky down the lantern, two chain lamps either side of the clock, round glass on the gallery's ceiling
    and in what is still let, one gas lamp in the court. Nothing in the bay under the break.
