1. **P** is a haunted railway terminus. The Phantom 666 carriage, exposed wheels, platform edge, and waiting room give it away. **Q** reads as a subway concourse, with broad stairs, a ticket window, and a gated central area.

2. **P is stronger overall, 4/5 versus Q’s 3/5.** P’s diagonal platform view leads the eye to the train, and the head-on doorway gives it a clear focal point. Ceiling lights pick out the platform, and the carriage and benches give it human scale. Q’s wide stairs and tiled floor convey public scale and make the concourse easier to read, but the evenly dim lighting and repeated grilles give the views similar weight.

3. **P has** a visible train undercarriage, numbered carriage, seated bodies in the car, and a waiting room with a clock and luggage. **Q has** a ticket window, broad stair flights, a long run of folding grilles, and a skull-topped post at the corner.

4. **Neither reads as a thin copy.** The closest resemblance is P’s row of green-backed carriage openings and Q’s green-backed grille bays, both broken up by heavy uprights. P uses that rhythm to show off the train, while Q uses it to divide the concourse.

5. **Blocking faults.** In P’s lower-right view, the blue area has too little edge definition to tell whether it’s a floor, water, or a drop from the tracks. **Q:** none visible.
