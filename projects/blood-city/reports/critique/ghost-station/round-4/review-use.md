2. Make boarding and departure read as one event. Align the platform light with the open doorway, then make the door slam and the carriage lights dip as the train pulls away on pickup.
3. Carry PHANTOM through the route. Put an arrow beside it on the board in 3 and repeat the name above the platform entrance in 1.
4. Vary the bodies visible through the windows. Leave one slumped below the sill and one upright facing the door. Clear the doorway sightline so the empty seat breaks the pattern.
5. Angle the RECALL button toward the arrival track. Give it a lamp that changes when pressed, and let the player see the returning headlight from the button.
