### What makes me stop

The hero is the carriage. I’d stop at the first long view down its windows (4), then again when the same green stripe and lit windows come out of the water (21–22). The empty seat should be the detail I remember between those two views.

### Five changes, in order

1. Frame the empty seat from the platform. Put the gun on the cushion under one working ceiling lamp, clear the sightline through that pane, and leave the adjacent dead passengers visible.
2. Give the pickup a departure beat. After the gun pickup, close the boarding door, let the carriage shudder in place for a moment, then move it.
3. Carry the wayfinding from tickets to train. Add a **PHANTOM → PLATFORM** strip beside the route board and repeat **PHANTOM** above the open boarding door.
4. Thin out the waiting-room gore. Remove the hanging body in 7, leave a single suitcase on a bench, and save the hanging figure in 14 for the aisle reveal.
5. Make the stop legible. Put **RECALL TRAIN** directly above the button in 19 and place it where pressing it leaves the player facing the track.
