# Station, round 4: comparison critique

**1. Which is better.** Set B wins, though not by much. It has two to three times the signs of life, but its own frames are uneven: ref_02 and ref_04 are bare corridors. Set B is the hand-made one. Its posters (Jo-Jo, Monkey Wrestling, Northbound Express), corpses, cultists, kicked cans and its shade falloff into black doorways (ref_01) are authored. They are not dressing assembled from a list. Set A spreads a checklist evenly: a barrel, a bench, a sign, a booth.

**2. What B has that A lacks.**
- **Signs of use.** ref_01 has bodies on the floor, cans and people standing in the hall. Set A has a couple of barrels and one cultist standing stiffly behind a bench (view_04).
- **Posters and wall printing.** ref_01 hangs posters at eye height. Set A's walls are bare brick everywhere.
- **Light.** ref_01 and ref_02 fall off into darkness, and their sky has cloud and fire glow. Set A is lit flat and even (view_01, view_04, view_06).
- **Sky.** ref_02 and ref_04 open onto a cloudy night. Set A shows a slot of red lava texture (view_03, view_07) that reads as a fire ceiling, not as sky.

**3. Thin copies, and what A adds.** The carriage (view_01, view_02) copies ref_04's grey/green ribbed hull, with less depth. ref_04 has curtained windows. Set A's windows are wooden door panels pasted into the green band. The tracks in view_03 copy ref_02's tracks. What Set A adds:
- a ticket booth with a clerk behind bars (view_08), which is good
- a walkable carriage interior with curtains (view_05)
- a waiting hall seen through gates (view_04)
- an exterior facade with a clock (view_09)

**4. Wrong size, floating, stretched, mechanical or placeholder.**
- **Locomotive** (view_06, view_07): a diamond-plate box on wheels, with no boiler, cab or chimney. It is a placeholder.
- **Station sign** (view_09): it reads "STATI" because the lettering is cut off by the window column.
- **Green lettering** (view_03 "…EEND", view_07 "THE QUAY"): it is clipped at a wall edge and floats in mid-air.
- **Carriage seats** (view_05): they are bare wooden crates.
- **Pillars and gates** (view_01, view_04): pillar-and-gate bays repeated at a fixed pitch.
- **Barrels** (view_02, view_06, view_07): the same barrel in every view.

**5. Score:** 2/5. The ticket window and the carriage interior are the only frames a player would remember.

**6. Blocking defects.**
- The facade sign is truncated to "STATI" (view_09).
- The "THE QUAY" and "…EEND" lettering is clipped at a wall edge and floats (view_03, view_07).
- The locomotive is a plain diamond-plate box with no cab, boiler or chimney (view_06, view_07).
- Wooden door panels stand in for carriage windows (view_01, view_02, view_04).
