# Recognition critique: station, round 1

**1. What is it?** An underground train platform with a carriage parked alongside. Sureness: 2. Frame 4 reads as something else, a city street with a narrow alley cut into a building. Frame 2 reads as a plain back corridor.

**2. Evidence and confusions.**
- For: a row of riveted iron columns running along one long edge, a brick barrel vault overhead, and a long, low object with a dark ribbed skirt along the floor edge. Together these hint at a platform with a car beside it.
- The "carriage" has no windows, doors, roof curve, wheels or ends. Its green band with grey rails looks like the felt on a giant billiard table, or a bar counter, or a hedge in a planter.
- The red glowing patch above it (frames 3, 5, 6) reads as lava or a hole in the ceiling, not as sky or light.
- The low brown row in frame 1 looks like a bench or a fence. Its purpose is unclear.
- The small pale sprites on the columns (frames 3 and 6) are too small to read. They could be posters or switches.
- The black opening in frame 1 is an unlit void.

**3. Expected but missing.** A platform edge with a drop to the track, rails and sleepers, a tunnel mouth at the ends, train windows and doors, benches, lamps, a clock, timetable or ticket hall, stairs up to the street, litter, and some light contrast. Everything here is lit at one even dimness.

**4. Needs to be told.** That the green slab is a train. That the street alley leads to a station. That this is a station at all rather than a pillared cellar or warehouse.

**5. Character score:** 2. The vault-and-columns look is decent, but nothing is memorable and nothing is specific to the place.

**6. Blocking defects.**
- The train has no windows, doors, wheels or ends, so it reads as a green felt wall or counter.
- There is no platform edge, track bed or rails, so nothing says "railway".
- The red ceiling patch over the train reads as lava or a rendering hole.
- The lighting is flat and uniform, with no lamps; the black doorway is an unlit void.
- The street entrance (frame 4) gives no sign or shape of a station entrance: no stairs and no canopy.
