A raised stone platform edge beside the rails with a barrel on it (2). The barred window with a figure behind it reads as a ticket office (8). The pillared hall with wooden counters and a robed figure looking out to the carriage (4, 5).
   What confused me: the carriage has no doors, no wheels you can see, no coupling and no roof shape. It is a box with stripes, and from the side (7) it reads as a green wall. In frame 1 the carriage windows look like holes in a building wall. The track in frame 3 runs into a black void under a lava-red sky. The counters in 4 and 5 are bare brown blocks that could be crates or pews.

3. I expect: a platform canopy with posts, benches, lamps, a station clock, luggage or trolleys, buffers or a tunnel mouth at the end of the line, real wheels, doors and windows with frames on the carriage, a locomotive, and a gate or turnstile between the hall and the platform.
