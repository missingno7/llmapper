# Station, round 3: comparison critique

## 1. Which is better
Set B, by a wide margin. B is the hand-made one. Its rooms have a point of view and a history: corpses on the floor, posters for Jo-Jo and monkey wrestling, cans rolled into corners, cultists placed to ambush (ref_01). Its light falls off into near-black (ref_02, ref_04). Set A is evenly lit, empty, and assembled from parts.

## 2. What B has that A lacks
- **Signs of use.** B has bodies, debris and rolled cans (ref_01), and bottles and glasses on the bar (ref_03). A shows bare floors in views 01, 04, 05 and 09.
- **Wall graphics.** B's posters and the "Express" plaques (ref_01, ref_02) tell you where you are. A has a single TICKETS plank (05, 08) and a green word you cannot read (03).
- **Lighting.** B's corridors fade to black, and fire glows on the sky (ref_02, ref_04). Every surface in A is lit at the same mid grey. Nothing is in shadow, and depth is never readable.
- **A believable train.** B's carriage has curtained windows, and a wooden balcony end with turned balusters (ref_04). A's carriage (01, 02, 04) has rectangular holes that show brick inside: a box with notches cut in it.
- **Surface variety.** B lays rug patterns, cobble against slab, and carved pillars. A repeats one grey slab and one dark-brick ceiling in every room.

## 3. Thin copies, and what A adds
- The carriage texture stack is lifted straight from ref_04, without the curtains or the balcony.
- The track bed copies ref_02, but the sky over A's tunnel mouth (03) is a red lava-like smear above a black void.
- The pillar-and-grate screen (04) copies ref_01, but nobody is behind it.
- A does add things B does not show: a ticket booth with a clerk behind bars (08), a waiting area with benches (04), and a street facade outside the station (09). These are real ideas, though each one is staged flat.

## 4. Scale, floating and repetition
- **Camera too close.** View 07 is jammed against the carriage corner. The texels are huge and the train reads as a crate.
- **Repeated grid.** The shop corridor (06) is one wooden shelf-grid tiled down both walls and the ceiling, with a crate dead centre blocking the view.
- **Placeholders.** The white paper blocks on the counters (05) and on the floor (01) read as placeholder cubes.
- **Train height.** The carriage sits on cobble with no visible wheels under most of it (02, 03), and its roof nearly scrapes the platform ceiling.
- **Cut-off sign.** The "ST..." lettering on the facade (09) runs off the wall.

## 5. Score
2 out of 5. The ticket booth is the only image a player would remember.

## 6. Blocking defects
- The carriage windows are empty recesses that show brick, with no glass, curtains or interior.
- Lighting is uniform everywhere, with no dark or bright areas.
- The track tunnel ends in a red lava sky above a black void.
- The shop corridor is one shelf texture tiled over walls and ceiling, with a crate blocking the aisle.
- There are no signs of use (bodies, debris, posters) anywhere on the platform.
