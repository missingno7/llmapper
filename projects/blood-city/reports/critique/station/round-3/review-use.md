## 2. Four things that would now do most
1. A station front at the door: a name board over it, a canopy, a lamp and a clock.
2. A locomotive that reads as steam: a chimney, a dome, a smokebox face and a tender. Today it is a 4.25 m wide box with a cab in the carriage's livery, 9 m long, and view_07 reads as a second carriage.
3. A waiting room with a grate, plus parcels and luggage on a trolley (fish boxes and trunks for a harbour).
4. Platform lamps on posts, a white-edged coping, a running-in name board, and a signal at the tunnel mouth.

## 3. Sizes and space

## 4. As a player
- The sequence: a cultist loiters in the hall. You enter the office by its side door, which puts you next to the zombie clerk, and pull the lever on the far west wall (OFFICE x = 6144). The carriage door is about 16 m away, through two walls, and nothing tells you it opened. In the carriage are a cultist and a zombie, and the prize, type 109 (a health item). Then you walk all the way back to the one street door.
- It has a beginning but no end. "Go on" (the platform ramp) leads to bars.
- What could break it: the door is remote-only with open_time 8. If that makes it close again, a player inside the carriage has no switch to get out.
- **Improvement 1:** put a second lever in the locomotive cab (or the carriage) that lifts the tunnel grille, so the line really does go TO THE QUAY.
- **Improvement 2:** make the lever also light the carriage (a shade change), and keep the door open (no return). The windows would then glow through the open gate bays as you come out of the office.

## 5. This town or any town?
It is generic. The Pandemonium Show bill, the sacks and barrels, and "TO THE QUAY" are the only ties to Gravesend. What would tie it: fish crates and ice on the platform, nets, a steamer or ferry timetable, sailors among the dead, harbour cranes visible beyond the far wall, and the line actually reaching the quay.

## 6. Score
2 out of 5. The booking window is memorable. The train and the dead end are not.

## 7. Blocking defects
- The carriage is a 5.1 m wide solid running from track bed to roof (6.7 m), and the boiler is a 4.25 m box. The train reads as buildings.
- The one way on, a tunnel signed TO THE QUAY, is barred. The station is a dead end.
- Pulling the office lever gives no feedback: the door it opens is about 16 m and two walls away.
- The carriage door is remote-only with open_time 8. If it closes again, a player inside is trapped.
- The street door is an unmarked slot. STATION is on the neighbour's wall, above eye level.
