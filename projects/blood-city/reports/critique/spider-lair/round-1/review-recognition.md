NAMED: Spider nest in a stone cellar. SURE 4, SCORE 3. Why: a web across the tunnel mouth, web-wrapped shapes hanging from the ceiling, niches each with an egg-shaped sac, dark rock ceiling, fieldstone walls and dirt floor. Odd: the egg sacs look like flat reliefs in neat framed niches (carved panels, tomb doors); the hanging cocoons stop short of the floor and read as floating columns; no webs on the walls or corners; laid masonry, not a cave; flat, even light.
