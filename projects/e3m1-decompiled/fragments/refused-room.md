# Fragment: refused-room

**What is this room for? Any word will do -- and 'nothing, it is just a room' is an answer.**

Layer 8 refuses to name 25 of E3M1's 36 grouped spaces: no measurement distinguishes them. The prop reader is now wired in and named 11 of them by their furniture. Sector 208 is one of the refusals, reached through the shopfront 206 -- the map's only opening. If the owner can name it and say from what, that is a measurement the reader does not have yet; if the owner cannot either, the refusal rate is the honest answer and stops being an open question.

Cut from E3M1: sectors **206, 208** — those are their ids in the whole map, and an answer that names one lands where it means something. In the fragment they are 0..1 in that order.

22 walls, 20 of them sealed where the cut runs (blocking, wearing tile 100, the tile this piece already shows on its own solid walls). 2 sprites. You start in sector 206 at the floor.

The sealed edges are the cut, not the map: ignore them. Everything inside them is E3M1's own geometry, untouched.
