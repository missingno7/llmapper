# Fragment: street-width

**Standing in the middle: is this street's width the CARRIAGEWAY you walk on, or the whole gap between the buildings?**

read_plan measures both and they land in different classes: E3M1's main street is 7.28 pu of carriageway (an avenue) and 10.78 pu including its pavements (a boulevard). The writer has to pick one to build to, and the classes were mined from the campaign on the full width. A census cannot say which number a body feels; the answer decides which width every street we author is sized by.

Cut from E3M1: sectors **1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 45** — those are their ids in the whole map, and an answer that names one lands where it means something. In the fragment they are 0..9 in that order.

61 walls, 33 of them sealed where the cut runs (blocking, wearing tile 384, the tile this piece already shows on its own solid walls). 64 sprites. You start in sector 1 at the floor.

The sealed edges are the cut, not the map: ignore them. Everything inside them is E3M1's own geometry, untouched.
