# Fragment: shade-step

**Walking from sector 8 to sector 3 to sector 2, is that ONE step of shadow getting deeper, or two?**

The reader recovers a light field of base + k*step. E3M1's own deltas are 8 -> 32 -> 34: one jump of 24 and one of 2. The campaign's median step is 13 (192 boundaries over 36 maps, quartiles [9, 18.75]), so the gate reads 24 as two steps and the writer would build it as two. No census can say what the eye does with 24 against 2 -- only looking can.

Cut from E3M1: sectors **1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 45, 159, 175, 235, 236** — those are their ids in the whole map, and an answer that names one lands where it means something. In the fragment they are 0..13 in that order.

147 walls, 105 of them sealed where the cut runs (blocking, wearing tile 400, the tile this piece already shows on its own solid walls). 270 sprites. You start in sector 1 at the floor.

The sealed edges are the cut, not the map: ignore them. Everything inside them is E3M1's own geometry, untouched.
