## Reproduced and repaired bugs

- SE11 first-operation direction was reversed: ST23 negates the spawn direction before moving. Door 50 dragged sector 389 loop 3 into a self-intersection at 8 of 16 sampled travel positions; the corrected swing has none. The earlier bathroom label for room 389 was wrong.
- The actual bathroom is room 152 with stalls 58/59 and door 61. Its stray SE11 sprite 487 has hitag 409, with no same-hitag ST23 operator. The converter invented a Push rotor, then attached the room entry signal to it. Pass 09 restores a static room, preserves entry TX/command and retires marker 7 without renumbering. Door 61, hitag 408, remains a mover. E1L2 room 262 had the same inert-controller bug.
- SE15 similarly adds 1024 to its authored angle before the first translation; the converter now uses that direction.
- Tagged Z doors used fixed times or a fitted GPSPEED curve without distance. They now use endpoint distance and Duke's extra*4 step at 30 Hz. SE31/32 use sector extra copied into sprite.yvel, not sprite shade. Linear Blood wave 1 replaces default cosine easing.
- Independent SE12 lighting overwrote mover controls in sectors 50, 61, 211 and 369 and the entry command in static room 152. Existing controls win; omitted independent lighting is diagnosed for manual treatment.
- SE10 hitag is a 30 Hz counter, not Blood tenths. Return timing is put on the open end of the motion, including the OFF end of state-1 rotations. Presence holds and precise in-flight timer phase remain approximations.
- SE20 marked slides moved a third vertex because both selected walls were flagged. Blood automatically carries point2, so only their connecting edge is now flagged. All 36 local-corpus stretch cases now agree with source geometry.
- ST30 rising rotations had their Z endpoints reversed for state-1 initialization; their angle also now respects the engine's integer shift. Independent floor/rotation durations remain explicitly approximate when unequal.
- SE13 expansion speed follows its source Z step, and source effector Z is preserved. Conflicting independent horizontal and explosive motion is diagnosed instead of replacing a rotation with Z-only motion.
