# Intake hall: what this level did, and why

How Blood builds a front office (E3M4:153, E6M1:79, and E1M3:316 beside
them) is on the shelf: `knowledge/blood/studies/front-office/notes.md`.
This page is the project's own: its boundary, its passes, its experiment.

The boundary is explicit in `places.json`. Portal growth joined these halls
to many neighbouring rooms, so its inferred room was unsuitable as an editing
boundary. This is a practical entrance to the quarry pumping works, inside a
guarded covert facility. The building should still feel like a workplace.

## Native layout findings

The core is sectors 479/480/481/482/485/486; 14 and 483/484/487-490 are collars
and thresholds; 0/1 are shaped entrance supports. Sector **148** was initially
misread as a counter. It has an enclosed outline, sloped floor/ceiling and
adjoining explosive sectors 146/147/149/150. It is excluded from this pass.

The visible low workbench is **404**, with moving/explosive pieces
390/403/405. Source SE13/SEENINE wiring remains meaningful evidence. No book,
chair or permanent decoration was attached to those pieces, and no geometry
or mechanism fields were changed. Trace their intended event before dressing.

## Experiment intake-01

`hypothesis.json` was written against the input hash before pass 04. The fixed
seven poses have matching before/after states. Pass 04 replaces the leaf floor
with slab tile 365 (catalog reading, no owner name), ceiling grid with
owner-named pressed metal 215, and oversized brickwork with owner-named brick
181. Pass 05 corrects the first render's streaked brick proportions and uses
owner-named riveted cladding 2453 for the former ornate frame strips.

Three flat ceiling fixtures use owner-named fluorescent light 2290. The clock
uses 1165 as decoration, with no switch behavior. Fixtures have no blocking
flags. The existing shade differences remain; fixtures are not evidence of
new dynamic illumination or a complete lighting pass.

Actors 119/189/190 are written at an ART-aware floor seat, changing only Z.
The sunk-actor authoring diagnostic should fall 72 → 69. Blood seats actors
at spawn, so this repairs the saved/editor presentation; it does not prove a
runtime combat fix. Existing encounter types, positions and extensions remain.

The room is an initial material/fitting pass. It still needs workbench purpose,
surface hierarchy at adjoining rooms and independent review. Layer counts and
changed pixels are diagnostics; neither is an aesthetic pass.

Continuation: the owner identified the workbench as a self-destructible tape
player. Its purpose and missing Use/delay sequence are now traced and repaired
in passes 06/07. See `briefing.md`; the observations above describe
the earlier intake-01 experiment, not the latest mechanical status.
