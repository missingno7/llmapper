## Route and source transfer

The original E1M3 gallery and E1M4 gate remain the short route to the exit.
A ten-sector curved stair from E1M2 supplies the long alternative:

Gallery -> native curved stairs -> upper perimeter hall -> upper rooms ->
new descending stair -> original goal/exit.

The upper rooms also open onto a four-panel wooden sprite overlook above the
gallery. It is a dead-end overlook with a return to the upper hall, not a second
stair down. The inherited gallery actors and supplies are retained and untuned.

The source stair sectors are 101, 100, 99, 98, 94, 95, 97, 96, 117 and 116.
Behavior closure stays within those ten sectors, with no unresolved gameplay
references. The run rises 36864 Z units through nine 4096-unit transitions.
The exact source hash is pinned by the recipe. Low source wall 951 is attached
to gallery wall 10; high source wall 836 connects to the authored upper hall.
Native allocation/transform reports are in the output's `report.json`.

The upper floor is -28672, above ground floor 8192. The gallery ceiling rises
to -61440 to provide standing headroom above the deck. Above the enclosed goal,
upper and lower sectors have distinct vertical bands. Above the open gallery,
the deck uses blocking floor-aligned sprites rather than an overlapping sector.
ART tile 256 was checked locally as 128x128. The last panel is lengthened by one
32-unit texel strip and shifted by 16 units to seat flush at the open upper portal;
all four panels touch edge-to-edge with no gap or area overlap.

## Evidence and corrections during construction

The first generated winding descent intersected itself; it was replaced with
separate rectangular stair arms and landings compiled through PlanarLayout.
The final native geometry is independently adapted into a layer inspection view.
Two pairs overlap in plan; both have disjoint vertical volumes and portal
separation. No layer errors or warnings remain. Collinear-wall notes stay visible.
The recipe additionally requires disjoint vertical bands for every overlap, even
where the general engine rules would permit overlap through graph separation.

Rendering exposed a rectangular missing area from the deck even after the static
layer warnings were resolved. The renderer identified a collinear pair between
the upper and lower goal rooms. Offsetting the upper room removed both the visible
rectangle and the reported unorderable pair in that same fixed witness view.
Seven named editor views were captured successfully; raw frame counters and any
remaining source-stair ordering observations are preserved in observation JSON.
A renderer status of `ok` alone is not treated as a quality guarantee.

Static route witnesses account for standing clearance (16960), maximum upward
step (4096), portal width (384 minimum), and blocking flags. They reach the upper
room and exit with the original gate closed, through the actual donor stairs.
Blocking the stair entry removes that route; the model cannot jump from the
lower gallery floor to the elevated portal. Sprite support is checked separately
through deck dimensions, continuity, flags and headroom, not credited as an
ordinary floor-level portal traversal.

Seven focused tests pass, covering deterministic rebuild, gate preservation,
material correction, channel remapping, broken controls, a blocked upper route,
vertical collision rejection and deck seating. Native validation is clean and
the new MAP passes a byte-exact encode/parse/encode roundtrip.
