# Study: meat-market

Written 2026-11-25, for the market hall of Gravesend (a supermarket of two
shelf banks with armor in it and nobody) and for the three bare wooden
platforms on the pavement before it, of which the owner said they "supposed to
represent something but it doesn't look like anything". Frames through
`tools.gallery` and a hand-aimed camera; read by me, by no blind reader.

## What I want to build

A meat market: one thing to remember from the street door (an aisle of hung
carcasses leading to a shut steel door), a change of level that is used (a
clerk's gallery over the floor, whose lever opens that door), what was shut in
with the meat, and the armor behind it. Outside, street stalls that say what
they are.

## Where Blood built it

- **E3M2 s258 and s306**, meat processing: 1351 at 104 by 88, twelve of them in
  one cluster hung from a dark plank ceiling (174) 3.9 bodies up, over a marble
  floor (253) with blood pools 1417/732/713; walls of metal panel 492, 494. Two
  butchers (204) under them. Several carcasses send when shot (vector) and a
  red or green spider (213) spawns under them.
- **TEDE1M2 room 532**, a butcher's kitchen: black and white chequer (292),
  grimy walls 80/89, a stone block in the middle with a circular saw (655)
  standing in it, pans, tools on the wall, and carcasses seen through a doorway.
  The room is 14 by 15 m and holds one block, one counter along two walls, and
  bare floor.
- **E1M3 s305**, the freezer: floor 315, ceiling 255, walls 345 whose lower
  third is white frost and grime; 1351 at 40, hung so their feet are at knee
  height; pools 1417 and 1413; blood drips (702, built running).
- **E3M2 s232**, plucked birds (1350) in a ring over a vat of blood.
- **DWE3M10 s286 and s287**, two street stands on a promenade: a counter round
  the front and one side, 2,048 wide; a roof of plank sprites (178, cstat 417
  and a one-sided underside) 1.8 to 2.05 bodies over the floor, its edge beams
  sprites of 68; a boarded back of 170. E6M1 sector 9 has the same roof of 21
  over a campaign street corner.
- **E1M4's midway**: a booth is a mouth in a striped front (1004, brown and grey
  stripes), a strip of bulbs (77) along its lip. The only striped cloth in the
  art that is not a curtain.

## What makes it read as that

- The meat hangs LOW. In every one of these rooms the feet of a carcass are
  between knee and head, so the eye is among the meat, not under it.
- One colour. Grey floor, grey or brown walls, and the carcasses the only thing
  lit and warm; the pools under them repeat it on the floor.
- The floor between is bare. E3M2's 12 m of marble has nothing on it but blood.
- A stand is read from its ROOF: without one, a counter in a street is a box.

## Sizes

- Carcass 1351: 57 by 133 texels; at 84 by 68 it is 1,197 wide and 2.1 bodies
  tall. E3M2's: 2.8 bodies in a room of 3.9; E1M3's: 1.25 in 1.5.
- E1M3's freezer 4.8 by 6.1 by 2.7 m. Ours 4.2 by 8.9 by 3.4 m.
- DWE3M10's stand: 2,048 by 768, roof at 1.8 bodies.

## What I will transcribe

- E1M3's freezer surfaces and its drips; E3M2's breakable carcass record (416,
  status 4, data_1 8, data_4 2205), its pools and its steel door (495).
- DWE3M10's stand: half-body counter, sprite roof, boards behind; its roof in
  E1M4's stripe, with a valance, so that it is a stall and not a shelter.

## What I will not do, and why

- No vat and no saw: E3M2's and TEDE1M2's own pictures.
- No spiders from the carcasses: the city has a spider lair of its own. Two of
  the hung men come down instead, when the armor is taken.
- No low ceiling for a stall's roof: under the city's sky a ceiling draws a wall
  from itself to the sky's top, eleven bodies up.
- Not three stalls. Two, at the plaza's west corners, and the door between them
  clear.

## What I still do not know

- Whether a player finds the gallery's lever unprompted: the steel door is on
  the aisle's axis and the stair is beside the back door. Nobody has walked it.
- What blind readers make of it: not run.
