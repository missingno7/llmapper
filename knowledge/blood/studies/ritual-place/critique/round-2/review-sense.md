# Sense, round 2 (Opus)

Score 3. Blocking, certain: the hole is a ruled rectangle; with the fires out
hard-edged wedges of light stay, the vault shows black and red polygons and the
cornice jumps from piece to piece; one tapestry is brighter than its twin.
Minor: dead torches stay brassy; the orb tints nothing; the window lights
neither reveal nor sill; carpet in two strips; a blood blob cut at a step; limbs
past the nosing; a hanged man standing; eye-column sides show sliced repeats;
gallery floor is street rubble. Well made: star lines meet the well's rim
exactly, the body hangs from the crown dead over the well.
