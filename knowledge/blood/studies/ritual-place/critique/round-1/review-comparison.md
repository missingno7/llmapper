# Comparison, round 1 (Opus; sets A ours, B E6M6, C E1M7 and DWE2M4, D SSR3, unlabelled)

Ranked A (ours) first of the four: "the only one where you see at once what is
worshipped, how and by whom". Not a thin copy of any. Score 4.

Blocking for quality: flat light ("floodlit": no visible pools, no dark edge);
the ceiling a vast plain sheet. Minor: the figure oversized and frozen; the
victim a hat in every key view; two or three poses repeated fifteen times; pale
niches; fangs like tents; few worshippers; candles in the way in; bluish marble
meets grey abruptly; too much symmetry; the heart's crosses. One change: near
black ceiling and upper walls, steep falloff.
