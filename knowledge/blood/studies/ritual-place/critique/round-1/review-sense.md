# Sense, round 1 (Opus)

Score 3. Blocking, certain: light ignores its sources (pale areas with hard
edges and no fire in them, torches with no pool); the event barely changes the
light; the heart floats. Uncertain: the window's teeth are kite-shaped and grey
in a flesh mouth. Minor: doll-sized niche body; candles half a cultist tall;
clean-cut hole; fang floors painted as triangles; carpet like paving; candles in
the way in; the figure's arms outside the well, its glow lights nothing; body
over the well touches its crown; well teeth a heap of slabs; niches brighter
than the brick; box without a parapet; flesh passage square and veined like
marble; burst window leaves no shards.

Found from this: the light groups were in the wrong mode. A lamp put out by an
OFF has to be a group that starts ON; as "out" groups they waited for an ON that
never came, so the fires went out and their light stayed.
