# Study: front-office

Written 2026-11-26 from the study the E4L1 remake made of its intake hall
(`projects/duke-e4l1-remake`, place `intake`), moved to the shelf so the next
level finds it. What that level built is in the project's `notes/intake.md`.

## What I want to build

A front office or reception hall of a working building: a desk or counter
where people arriving are received, beside the way through, that reads as
a workplace and not as a monument.

## Where Blood built it

- **E3M4, sector 153** (seed `E3M4:153!`): the hospital's admission hall,
  30 by 31 m and 10.4 m high. The counter is a destination at the side of
  the circulation, not in its way.
- **E6M1, sector 79** (seed `E6M1:79!`): an office, 6 by 14 m, 3.5 m high:
  long wooden counters (wood 34, drawer fronts 36), chairs, a wall clock
  (1165), a fluorescent fitting (2290) on a ceiling grid, posters.
- **E1M3, sector 316** (seed `E1M3:316!`) is NOT an office: it is a crate
  store (crates 95 and 452, riveted cladding 2453, metal plate floor 491).
  It is here as the restrained work palette next door to an office.
- **E6M1, sector 91** is catalogued as the office, but its own view is a
  crate closet: a catalogue seed names a cluster, and the one sector must
  be looked at before it is taken for the place.

## What makes it read as that

1. **Every surface has one job.** In E3M4 the floor (chequer 290), the
   piers, the opening and the counter differ in material; nothing is
   decorated for its own sake.
2. **Furniture of use**: counters of wood 34 with drawer fronts 36 as their
   faces; a chair at a counter; a clock on the wall.
3. **Practical light**: a fluorescent fitting (2290) in a ceiling grid over
   the desk, in a low room (3.5 m).
4. **Paper on the walls**: posters and notices (words: a blind reader is
   shown the room without them).

## Sizes

- E6M1's office: 3.5 m to the ceiling, counters about a third of a body
  deep and waist high; E3M4's hall is a public building, ten metres high.
- A counter wants a body's aisle on the public side.

## What I will transcribe

- Wood 34 counters with drawer fronts 36, a chair, a wall clock 1165 as
  decoration (no switch), a fluorescent 2290 overhead.
- E3M4's order: the desk beside the way through, the floor, piers and
  counter in different materials.

## What I will not do, and why

- E3M4's lettering and chequered floor in a works office: they say
  hospital.
- E1M3's crates as furniture: they say store, and they block the room.

## What I still do not know

- Whether a reader names a desk room an office without its posters.
- How Blood shows a guard post at an entrance; no seed of one was found.
