NAMED: Spider nest cave with egg sacs. SURE 4, SCORE 3. Why: pale ribbed egg sacs in alcoves, three clustered in a webbed recess under a spider emblem; webbing on lintels and across a tunnel mouth; mossy stone, dark earth. Other: reads as a dungeon of square rooms and a pillar, not a cave; flat grey ceiling; webbing sparse; the hanging pale funnels ambiguous; cocoons look like grey statues; dim even light; no sense of a centre.
