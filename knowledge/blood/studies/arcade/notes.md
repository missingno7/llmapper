# Study: arcade

## What I want to build

Gravesend's shopping arcade, half closed, in place of a straight hall between six equal shops that a blind reader named
"Ornate hallway with library alcove". One thing must make it an arcade before any sign is read, the floors must stand at
different heights, the shops must differ, and the eye key must be the reward of going through it.

## Where Blood built it

E4M9, "Mall of the Dead", is the campaign's only mall: the atrium s97, the basin and its shaft s89/s90, two escalators
(s79-s83, s128), the gallery over the atrium (s103/s104, s84-s86), K Marche (s62, s66) and shuttered shops along the
gallery. E6M1:47 is the campaign's shop. E2M4:613 (the Overlooked Hotel's bar under a balustrade) is how Blood looks down
on a big room. TEDE1M5:384 is a bookshop. DWE3M4:343 is a Death Wish amusement arcade with one glass box in its middle.

## What makes it read as that

* **A room seen across at two heights.** From E4M9's atrium floor the gallery's openings are seen, and from the gallery
  the atrium: frames `E4M9_97`, `E4M9_103`. The shops are behind that, not what it is made of.
* **An escalator.** Ribbed metal 376 on one sloped sector (heinum -2816, 34 degrees), a rail 195 sloped with it 8,192
  higher and 256 wide, its side one wall of 255. Nothing else is needed and nothing else says mall as fast.
* **A basin under a hole in the roof.** A marble rim 43 a step over the floor, stagnant water, and over it the sky in a
  shaft: the one bright thing in a grey room.
* **Paving 44, banded plaster, two banners, a round clock over a way out.** Almost nothing stands on the floor: the
  atrium's middle is empty, and that is why it is big.
* **Shut shops are cheap and say more than open ones**: a grille (465) or a gate in the wall, a name board over it
  (740, 1145, 2340, 2341), nobody. E4M9 has five along its gallery and two that can be gone into.
* A shop is one wall of its goods: TEDE1M5 is bookcases and a desk, E6M1 is shelves and a counter. It is recognised from
  the door because the goods are the wall.

## What I will transcribe

The escalator (376, 195, 255, slope and rail), the basin's rim and its shaft of sky, paving 44, the banners 845/847 and
the clock over the way out, the shut shop's name board over its gate, 588 as a free-standing balustrade (E2M4 sprites
181-196), a bookshop whose walls are books (TEDE1M5).

## What I will not do, and why

* No E4M9 plan: its atrium is an octagon with the escalator beside it. Ours is a gallery on two sides, and it is broken:
  the way round has fallen in, so the floor it fell on has to be crossed.
* No room over a room: the gallery and the court lie side by side in plan, the shops of each level behind it.
* No grey plaster 369 on every wall: the marble and green pilaster 1718 (E1M1, DWE3M4) with the green coffer 454 the
  arcade already had over it, and the papered 108 kept for the low gallery, so the hall is cool and the gallery warm.
* No row of shops. Three: one lit and kept (books), one shut until it matters (guns), one looted (the draper's); one more
  is a gate in the wall.

## What I still do not know

Whether a body at full run clears the gap in the gallery (1.3 plan units). If it does it is a short cut to the key, and
the fight is the same.
