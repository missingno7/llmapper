# Study: Ghost Station

## What I want to build

A tiled underground platform with the lights still on, reached by a concealed
maintenance hatch at the foot of Gravesend's pump-station spiral.

## Where Blood built it

E1M2 s272 is the platform beside the track cutting. E1M2 s264–266 carry the
track into a dark covered run. The plans and posed views are generated from
the two seeds in [study.json](study.json). E3M2 sprites 669–671 supply the
four-position industrial dial. The matching circuit is the one in
`#SPR22`, and the single moving panel follows `DOOR-CLOSET` s17.

## What makes it read as that

Both rail views have a continuous line of sleepers and a strong vanishing
point. On the platform, pillars divide the long passenger edge from the
track, while at the tunnel the roof closes over the rails and hides the
distance. A name at passenger height and benches on the dry side give the
abandoned room a recognisable former use.

## Sizes

The two sampled runs measure 1.7 × 20.9 m and 6.1 × 20.0 m. Gravesend needs
one short platform and one track, with enough width to turn at the stair
and pass a bench. The approach aperture is 1,280 Build units wide.

## What I will transcribe

Use the track-and-edge silhouette, the grey-brick tunnel, working pools of
light and the campaign's wall lettering. Make the platform's tiles and
timber seats distinct from the sewer on the other side of the hatch.

## What I will not do, and why

The track ends at a recessed brick wall. The legend describes a closed line
and the platform supplies the reward. The service stair doubles as the way
back to the pump landing.

## What I still do not know

The observer can show the travel endpoints and the appearance of the
platform. The feel of turning the dials and hearing the latch belongs to a
walk in Blood.
