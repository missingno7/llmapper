**Five changes, in order**

1. Give the gun its own unmistakably empty seat. Leave the full seat cushion and back visible from the aisle, keep limbs off it, and put a ceiling light directly over it.
2. Stage the reveal across three windows. Show one upright passenger at the gate, keep the next window dark, and let boarding reveal the full row of dead.
3. Add an arrow beside PHANTOM on the board and carry the existing floor stripe through the gate to one lit open carriage door.
4. Remove the RUN lettering and lever from the back wall. Put a lamp over the boarding door that goes out when the gun is taken, giving the departure a visible first beat.
5. Put RECALL beside the arrival-side platform edge, facing the track the train returns on. Light the button and the empty berth together so I can watch what I’ve called.

**Overall · 3/5. BLOCKING:** The empty seat doesn’t read from the aisle, so the gun pickup loses the moment when the player realizes the train has a place for them.
