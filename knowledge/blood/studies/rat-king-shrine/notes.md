## What I want to build

The Rat King's Shrine. It is a legend secret off the Stockyard Drain: the toshers' tale of a knot of rats bound by their tails, worshipped in the dark. You enter through a low pipe off the drain's east walk. Inside, candles stand round a stepped stone altar with the rats on a blood mark, and an offering sits before them. Crossing into it counts the level's sixth secret.

## Where Blood built it

- **DWE1M5:610** (community-curated). Candles and remains on stone: seven rats (1745), gib remains (505, 690, 563).
- **E1M4:167**. The rats on the grill: rat decorations (1743, 1744) beside two life essences (2169), in dark stone (255).

## What makes it read as that

- **A worshipped thing.** The altar is raised in two steps (3,072 and 6,144) at the end of the room, lit only by candles.
- **The rats as an idol.** Three of E1M4's rat sprites stand close on E2M4's blood mark (713), with E1M5's skull plaque (187) above them.
- **A way in that is a pipe, not a door.** The low throat is in tunnel tile 194, with a single candle inside it.
