# Study: toshers-den

## What I want to build

The Toshers' Den, off the settling hall's west quay: where the sewer's scavengers slept. A low warm room of timber after all the steel, with a stove, lanterns, straw beds and a few finds; a rest before or after the Cabal's chamber. And the Toshers' Cache, a secret the campaign's own way.

## Where Blood built it

- **E6M7:195 and E6M7:108.** Rooms shored with timber (172, 170, beams 173) over rough stone, 11.3 x 13.9 m and 3.5 m high, the timber drawn warm by a hanging fire. The warmest rooms underground in the corpus.
- **DWE2M5:292.** A hay barn (Death Wish): plank walls (28, 172), a hay floor (514), boxes (562) as steps, barrels (925), spades and a broom (1713, 1710) leaned on the wall, one lantern on a chain in the middle.
- **E3M3:265.** The sewer's own squat: alcoves of straw (515) half a body up, a tommy gun and tins left lying. **E3M3:264** is a bed, and the secret: a Z-motion sector 5,120 high that sinks to the floor when it is pushed (`trigger_wall_push`), its straw going down with it, and behind it E3M3:298, a slot as high as the bed was, with beast vision in it that counts the secret when it is picked up.
- **E3M3:16.** The other squat: straw, bodies, nine rats; a plank ceiling over stone.

## What makes it read as that

- **Timber on every wall**, where the corridor is stone and steel, and a plank roof low enough to touch.
- **A light somebody carries**: a brass lantern, a fire behind an oven door. Never a tube.
- **Beds**: straw at the height of a box, in a row.
- **Tools leaned on the wall**, barrels in a corner; the middle of the floor is empty.

## Sizes

- E6M7:195: 32,768 high. Ours is lower, 26,624 (a body and a half): it is a place for sleeping.
- Beds 1.4 by 0.75 plan units (2.4 by 1.3 m); two of them one box (6,144) up, the wrong one two boxes (10,240).
- A crouching body clears 13,376. The bed sinks 4,096 past the floor, so the crawl behind it is 14,336 high and no higher than the bed at rest; one step to climb back.

## What I will transcribe

- E3M3:264's bed: type 600, pushed by its wall, `dude_lockout`. Ours moves once and stays (E3M3's comes back up after four seconds, onto whoever is inside).
- Tiles: 172 walls, 178 floor, 21 roof, 169 beams and lintels, 562 and 514 for the beds, 261 for the oven's door (the owner: "small door, like a furnace's") under an iron throat (376), 693 for the lanterns, 1713, 1712, 1710 and 925.
- E6M7:195's firelight: three lanterns and the stove, nothing from the ceiling.

## What I will not do, and why

- No hay over the whole floor: it is a sewer, the hay is in the beds.
- No foes in it. It is a rest.
- No crack, no switch and no crooked panel for the cache: the bed is higher than its neighbours, which is the campaign's way of saying so (a copy of a thing that repeats, one of them wrong).
- No sliding bed: a sector that slides sweeps what lies beside it; E3M3's own only sinks.

## What I still do not know

- Whether the bed is found without being told. The owner will say.
