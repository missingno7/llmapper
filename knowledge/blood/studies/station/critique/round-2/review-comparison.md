# Station critique, round 2: comparison

**1. Verdict.** Set B is clearly better, by a wide margin (about 2 points on 5). I believe Set B is the hand-made one. Its places have been used and fought in. ref_01 has corpses, gibs, a dropped can, two cultists and three posters (Jo-Jo, Monkey Wrestling, Northbound Express). ref_03's bar carries bottles and glasses on its counters, with tapestries and a patterned carpet. ref_02 and ref_04 open onto a burning sky. Set A is assembled from the right parts, but those parts are clean, empty and evenly lit.

**2. What B has that A lacks.**
- Sky. Every B track and train view (ref_02, ref_04) is open to a smoky, burning parallax sky. Every A view has a flat brown plank ceiling, even over the track (view_02, view_03). That makes A a shed, not a station.
- Signs of use. There are no bodies, debris, cans, blood or litter anywhere in A. B has them in every sheet.
- Signage density. B has three posters and a station plaque in one hall. A has one TICKETS board (view_05, view_08), a torn "Possum" poster (view_06) and a stray green "ST" (view_09).
- Train detail. B's car has curtains in its windows and an end balcony with turned balusters (ref_04). A's car has bare punched holes that show the brick wall behind it (view_01, view_02). It has no curtains and no balcony.
- Lighting. B has dark gates against lit floors and deep shade. A is a single grey-brown level of light everywhere.
- Goods and props. B's bar (ref_03) is full of things. A's halls hold only box benches (view_04).

**3. Thin copies and originals.** The train livery copies ref_04 (green band, ribbed skirt, bogie trim) with less of it, and the gated pillar hall in view_04 copies ref_01 with no posters and no bodies. A does two things B does not show. It has a ticket booth with a barred window and a clerk (view_08), and a street facade outside the station (view_09). Both are good ideas.

**4. Scale and placeholders.**
- The butcher in view_06 stands on a crate in the middle of a corridor and fills the frame. He looks like a statue on a plinth.
- The benches in view_04 and view_05 are solid red boxes. They read as placeholders.
- The shelf-cell walls in view_06 repeat one unit mechanically down both sides.
- The track tunnel in view_03 ends in a black void.
- The train in view_07 is jammed against a brick wall with no clearance.
- The train windows show brick through them.

**5. Score for Set A: 2/5.**

**6. Blocking defects in Set A.**
- A solid ceiling covers the tracks and train, so there is no sky or open air.
- There are no signs of use (no bodies, debris or litter).
- The train windows are holes onto brick, with no curtains or interior.
- The light level is flat across every view.
- The benches are bare boxes, and the halls have almost no props.
- The track tunnel ends in a black void.
