# Station round 4 — physical sense

## Faults
- **v06/v07, locomotive has no chimney and no clear front (sure 2).** It is a diamond-plate box with a stepped top: a low end, then a wide tall block (v07 x400-480) that reads as neither a cab nor a chimney. I can see three coupled driving wheels, so it is meant to be steam, but nothing marks which end leads. A steam loco without a chimney is not a working engine.
- **v01/v04 vs v05, the carriage's outside does not match its inside (sure 2).** Outside, each carriage shows only one or two small brown wooden panels with a dark inset, which read as doors or shutters. Inside (v05), both walls are a continuous run of tall glazed windows with curtains. A player who boards will see windows that are not there from the platform.
- **v03, platform height (sure 1).** The camera stands in the trackbed, and the platform top sits at about eye level, roughly 1.5 m above the rails. The carriage then rises only about one more platform-height above that. The platform is high and the carriage low, but camera pitch could explain it.
- **v03, a rail appears to run beside the carriage rather than under it (sure 1).** This is ambiguous at this distance. In v02 the rail clearly runs into the carriage's end, so it is probably fine.
- **v09, the STATION letters run across the window mullions (sure 1).** The lettering looks painted over the glazing. That is odd but possible.

## Checked and right
- Rails run along the line and sleepers lie across them (v02, v03, v07). The rails continue into a tunnel mouth at the far end (v03).
- The carriage is in line with its track: the rail runs into its end, and its wheels are below the body (v02).
- The carriage runs parallel to the platform, its doors and windows face the platform, and the train continues in line (v01, v02, v04, v07).
- The loco's driving wheels are joined by a coupling rod, and their lower halves sit below the platform edge, which is consistent with standing in the trackbed (v06, v07).
- Curtains hang inside the windows, the floor is carpeted, and the seats are at seat height (v05).
- Nothing stands in the train's path. The barrels rest on the platform floor and are human-sized (v01, v02, v06, v07).
- The benches in the hall face each other across the aisle, and the seated figure sits on a seat facing out (v04). The iron gates sit between pillars, in the openings (v04).
- The ticket window faces the hall at counter height, with the clerk behind its bars. The TICKETS sign above it is upright and reads correctly. The door beside it is open and leads into a room with a floor (v08).
- On the street front (v09), the entrance arch leads into the building, the lamps flank and light the entrance, and the clock is upright. The letters are not mirrored.
- Light comes from a red sky through an opening over the tracks (v03) and from the lamps by the entrance (v09).

## Score
4/5. The earlier rails-across fault is gone, and the track, platform, building and fixtures all work. The remaining faults are the engine's identity and the carriage's windows.

## Blocking
- The locomotive has no chimney and no readable front; it is a featureless box on wheels.
- The carriage's outside shows one or two wooden panels per side, but inside it is lined with curtained windows.
