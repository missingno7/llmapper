# Round 5, recognition reader

**1. First read:** a small covered railway station with a passenger car and a steam engine at the platform. Sureness 4. Views 5 and 9 read as different places: 5 is a train-car interior that could also be a hotel corridor, and 9 is a street outside the station.

**2. What made me say it, and what confused me.**

What says "station":
- Two rail tracks on cobbles (3, 2).
- A long green-banded carriage with small windows (1–4).
- A dark engine with spoked wheels and a smokestack (6, 7).
- Wooden benches between iron columns with a waiting figure (4).
- Barrels on the platform.
- A barred window with a man behind it, which reads as a ticket booth (8).
- A clock on a big facade outside (9).

What confused me:
- Platform 1 (1) is a narrow brick alley between the wall and the carriage. It reads as a service passage, not a platform.
- The engine (6) is a grey stone-panelled block. Without the wheels I would call it a crypt wall or a furnace. The gargoyle head on it (7) reads as a temple ornament.
- The open strip of red sky over the tracks (3, 7) reads as hell or lava. It does not read as a station roof.
- The barred window in 8 also reads as a jail cell.
- The carriage window in 1 shows brick and woodwork, while the carriage interior in 5 is carpet and curtains. The two disagree.
- I see no doors on the carriage side.

**3. What I expected and did not see.** A raised platform edge, a train shed roof (trusses, glass) instead of a low cellar plank ceiling, hanging lamps, luggage or crates, buffer stops, steam or smoke, a platform clock, and a long platform where you see the train end to end.

**4. What needs imagination.** That the grey box is a locomotive. That the black hole at the end of the tracks (3) is a tunnel: it is a flat void with no portal. That the barred window sells tickets. That corridor 5 is inside that carriage.

**5. Character score: 3.** The carriage plus the bench hall is memorable. The rest is generic brick corridors.

**6. Blocking defects:**
- The locomotive reads as a stone block or crypt wall, not an engine.
- The main platform is an alley-width brick corridor, not a platform.
- The open red sky over the tracks reads as hell or lava, not a station roof.
- The carriage has no visible doors, and its window shows brick that contradicts the carpeted interior.
- The tunnel mouth is a flat black void with no portal or frame.
