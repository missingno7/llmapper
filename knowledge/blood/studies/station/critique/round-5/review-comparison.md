# Station, round 5: Set A against Set B

**1. Which is better.** Set B is the hand-made one. It looks lived in, and you can tell from its signs of use: corpses lie where a fight happened (ref_01), cultists stand at their posts, a beer can rolls on the floor, and there are circus posters with real copy ("JO-JO", "MONKEY WRESTLING"). It also has a bar with bottles and glasses set along the counter (ref_03), and the "Northbound Express" plaque in ref_02. Set B is not uniformly better, though. ref_02 and ref_04 are bare corridors with a single train beside them. Set A has more parts of a station and more varied surfaces. Overall B wins by a small margin, through narrative and life, not through construction.

**2. What B has that A lacks.** Set A has no aftermath and no clutter of use. B's ref_01 hall carries posters at eye level, bodies, litter and enemies grouped at a gated arch. A's equivalent, the waiting hall in view_04, has one hooded figure on a bench, one poster and one stray white post. Set A also has no light pooling. Every frame is flat mid-shade, with no lamp and no dark corner, so the platforms read as evenly grey. B's ref_02 has a sky with fire in it and a train light at the tunnel end. A's tunnel mouth in view_03 is a black box.

**3. Thin copies, and what A adds.** The carriage (grey ribbed skirt, green band, roof trim) is lifted directly from ref_04, as are the cobble track bed and rails from ref_02. A goes beyond B in several places: a furnished carriage interior with curtains, chairs and a zombie (view_05), a locomotive with wheels and a stack (views 06 and 07), a manned ticket window (view_08), and a street frontage with "STATION" lettering and a clock (view_09). B shows none of these.

**4. Size, floating, repetition, placeholders.**
- view_07: the "...THE QUAY" sign text floats in the air beside the gargoyle head and runs into the wall.
- view_07: a gargoyle head bolted onto the locomotive's front is decoration with no reason to be there.
- view_03: the green "TICKETS"-like text floats inside the booth with nothing behind it.
- view_03 and view_07: the red lava texture used as the sky over the track trench reads as a low red lid.
- view_02: thin stray line sprites lie on top of the carriage roof.
- Barrels are the only loose object anywhere. They appear in views 01, 02, 03, 06 and 07, the same barrel stamped again and again.
- view_04: the poster sits over the carriage side, between two pillars.

**5. Score for Set A:** 3 out of 5.
