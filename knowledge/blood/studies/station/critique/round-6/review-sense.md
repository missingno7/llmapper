# Station round 6 — physical sense (reader: sense)

## Faults
1. **view_09 (and view_08)**: the locomotive's driving wheels stand directly on the same stone paving the player walks on. No rails or sleepers can be seen under it, although rails do show beyond its front at right. A locomotive cannot stand on flagstones. Sure: 2.
2. **view_04**: the carriage does not clearly sit centred on a track. One rail comes out from beside its near-right corner and runs off past the station fence, and its partner rail is not visible. It reads as "a carriage beside a rail" rather than one on it. Sure: 1.
3. **view_01, view_05, view_06**: a "JO-JO in person" bill is pasted on the outside of the carriage, between a window and the door. Bills go on station walls, not on rolling stock. Sure: 2. Minor.
4. **view_09**: a horned gargoyle mask is fixed to the smokebox front, where the headlamp or buffer beam belongs. This is a dressing choice, not a mechanism, but it hides what the front end is. Sure: 1.
5. **view_06**: the green lettering on the upper-left wall runs vertically down the wall. A sign meant to be read from the concourse would run horizontally. The frame is too close and dark to read it properly. Sure: 1.
6. **view_05**: a thin bare post stands on the platform edge just in front of the carriage side. If it is a lever or a signal, it is very close to the train. Sure: 1.

## Checked and right
- Rails run along the train in view_03 and view_04. The centre track goes on into a dark tunnel mouth, and sleepers lie across the rails, not along them. They are not laid across the train any more.
- The carriage has dark bogie wheels under its end (view_03), and its long axis runs parallel to the platform.
- The locomotive is at the end of the train with the carriage behind it (view_09). The tall chimney is at its front, and a smoke streak rises above it (view_08, view_09).
- The carriage door is on the platform side with a sill at platform level and a handle (view_01). You could step in.
- Carriage windows sit at seated-eye height. Inside (view_07) there are curtains at every window, chairs at tables, and a window looking out onto the platform.
- The painted bands (grey, white stripes, green) run level along the whole side and continue either side of the door and windows (view_01, view_02).
- The platform edge is right beside the carriage, and nothing stands in the train's path on the platform (view_02).
- The barrels stand on the floor (view_03, view_08, view_09).
- The TICKETS sign is upright above a barred ticket window that faces the concourse. The door next to it opens into a room (view_10).
- The gate grilles fill the openings between the pillars (view_05).
- The STATION lettering on the façade faces the street and reads the right way round. The clock and the entrance face the street (view_11).
- Light: daylight comes from the red sky openings above the track (view_04, view_09). The interior light is plausibly from the windows.

## Score
4/5. The track orientation is fixed. The one fault a player would notice is the locomotive standing on paving with no rails.
