# Station, round 6: comparison critique

**1. Which is better.** Set B, though not by much: call it half a point. B is the hand-made one. It has the campaign's quirks: a dark navy-brick ceiling over a lit floor, posters lighting up a dim hall (ref_01), a burning city behind a cloud sky at the end of the tracks (ref_02), and a bar with odd triangular counters crowded with bottles and glasses (ref_03). Its light has contrast and its places feel inhabited. Set A has more *kinds* of thing in it, but it reads as an inventory, lit evenly and flatly.

**2. What B has that A lacks.**
- **Light falloff.** ref_01's ceiling goes nearly black while the posters stay readable. A's halls (view_05, view_06) sit at one mid shade from floor to ceiling.
- **A sky that is a sky.** ref_02 and ref_04 open onto clouds and fire. A's track shaft (view_04, view_09) is capped with a red marbled slab.
- **Small clutter on surfaces.** ref_03 has bottles, glasses and a key on its counters. A's counters and benches are bare.
- **Signs of use.** B has litter cans on the floor (ref_01) and a balcony railing over the train (ref_04).
- **Floor patterning.** ref_03's rug leads the eye down the room. A's floors are a single tile everywhere except the dining car.

**3. Thin copies, and what A adds.**
- The green-and-grey carriage, the Jo-Jo poster and the iron gates are lifted straight from B (ref_01, ref_04).
- A adds things B never shows: a ticket window with a sign and bars (view_10), a locomotive with wheels and a boiler (view_08, view_09), a furnished dining car with curtains, chairs and a table (view_07), and a street frontage with STATION lettering and a clock (view_11). View_07 is the best frame in either set.

**4. Wrong size, floating, mechanical or placeholder.**
- The benches (view_05, view_06) are backless solid crates.
- The posters are glued to the outside of the carriage (view_01, view_05, view_06), so the carriage is being used as a wall.
- The carriage windows are identical inset boxes repeated at a fixed pitch (view_02).
- The barrels are one sprite stamped down every corridor (view_03, view_08, view_09).
- A gargoyle head hangs off the locomotive's nose, and the sign "...TO THE QUA..." runs into it (view_09).
- The station doorway (view_11) is a flat black hole.

**5. Score: 3/5.** The dining car and the locomotive would stick in a player's memory. The halls would not.

**6. Blocking defects.**
- Posters are mounted on the outside of the train carriage (view_01, view_05, view_06).
- The station entrance doorway renders as a flat black void (view_11).
- The enclosed track shaft is lidded with a red marbled ceiling that reads as lava, not sky (view_04, view_09).
- The wall sign text collides with the gargoyle sprite on the locomotive (view_09).
