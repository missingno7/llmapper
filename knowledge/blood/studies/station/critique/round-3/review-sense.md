# Station round 3: physical-sense check

## Faults
- **view_01, view_04: train windows show red brick right behind the glass.** A carriage is hollow and you should see seats or the far windows through it. Brick right behind the glass makes it a wall with a window frame painted on. Sure: 2.
- **view_02, view_03, view_07: the train is one uniform box.** No locomotive, no cab, no front or back, no couplings between cars. Nothing shows which way it would move or what pulls it. Sure: 2.
- **view_07: the train's end stops a step short of a brick wall.** I cannot see the track running on past it. If the line ends here, it needs a buffer stop. The view is too close to settle this. Sure: 1.
- **view_01, view_02: the body skirt runs down to the ballast and hides the wheels.** Only an ornamental band and one spoked wheel (view_07) are visible. The body seems to sit on the ground, not on bogies over the rails. Sure: 1.
- **view_04, view_05: no lamp anywhere in the concourse or under the canopy,** but the light is even everywhere. The hall is roofed, so it can't be sky light. Sure: 2.
- **view_04: a thin white post stands at the platform edge, very close to the car side.** It may be in the train's swept path. Sure: 1.

## Checked and found right
- **Rails (view_02, view_03):** two rails per track. They run along the train's long axis toward the far tunnel and meet at the same vanishing point as the train body. Sleepers lie across the rails. The rail gauge is plausible for the car's width. The train sits on the right-hand track: its rails come out from under the near end. The left track is empty and runs into a dark tunnel mouth. The earlier rails-across-the-train fault is gone.
- **Platform (view_01, view_02):** the edge runs right beside the car side, at about the level of the car floor. Doors and windows are on the platform side. The windows (view_01) are at the right height for a seated or standing passenger.
- **Ticket office (view_08):** the barred window faces the hall at counter height, with the clerk behind it. The TICKETS sign is above it, upright, and readable from the hall. The door next to it opens into a room.
- **Counters (view_05):** they face the hall, with staff behind them. The paper sits on the counter top.
- **Gates (view_04):** the iron gates hang in the openings between columns, not in front of solid wall.
- **Barrels (view_02, view_03):** they stand on the platform floor, upright and at the right size.
- **Shelving aisle (view_06):** the crate stands on the floor. The figures are human-sized. The poster is upright.
- **Street (view_09):** the facades have windows at storey height. The passage between the buildings is a real opening. The sign is upright.
- **Sky (view_03):** it appears only above the open slot over the tracks.

## Score
3/5. The track and building logic now work. The train itself does not read as a working vehicle.
