# Station, round 1: comparison critique

**1. Verdict.** Set B is much better, by a wide margin. I take B to be the hand-made one. Its ref_01 hall has posters with jokes ("JO-JO the idiot circus boy", "Monkey Wrestling"), cultists and corpses, dropped cans, iron gates and a "Northbound Express" sign. Its ref_02 has real rails on ballast under a burning sky. Set A has the same textures with none of that authorship.

**2. What B has that A lacks.**
- **Track.** ref_02 shows rails, sleepers and a cobble track bed. No image in A shows a rail. The train in view_03, view_05 and view_06 sits flush against the platform floor, with no edge and no drop.
- **A train that reads as a vehicle.** ref_04 has curtained windows and a wooden end-balcony with a railing. A's train is a blank green band on a grey wall: no windows, no doors, no end.
- **Signs of use.** ref_01 has corpses, cans, cultists and posters. A has one tiny poster on a pillar (view_03, view_06) and nothing on the floor.
- **Sky and depth.** B shows a fire-lit cloud sky over the tracks. A closes everything under a low brick vault.
- **Surface variety.** B has cobble, wood, carpet (ref_03), gates and tapestries. A has one grey flag floor everywhere and one brick.
- **A social room.** ref_03 has a bar with bottles, glasses and carpets. A has nothing like it.

**3. Thin copies, and what A does that B does not.**
- **Thin copies.** A's pillar row and brick-vault hall (view_01, view_03) copy ref_01's riveted pillars with none of its dressing. A's green-band train is ref_04's car with its curtains and balcony removed.
- **A's own move.** view_04 frames a street facade with windows opening into a passage toward the platform. That gives an arrival from the town, which B does not show.

**4. Wrong size, floating, mechanical or placeholder.**
- "TICKETS" (view_01) is tiny orange text floating on a bare wall above a low row of benches, with no window or booth. It reads as a placeholder.
- The pillars are identical and evenly spaced (view_03, view_06).
- There are red/black sky slivers at the train-to-vault seam (view_03, view_05, view_06). The ceiling does not meet the train.
- view_01 has a black void doorway.
- view_02 is a long, featureless stone alley.
- The train rises into the ceiling, so it reads as a wall, not a carriage.

**5. Score for Set A: 2/5.**

**6. Blocking defects.**
- No track, rails or platform edge; the train sits on the platform floor.
- The train is a flat wall with no windows, doors or end.
- Sky leaks at the train/ceiling seam.
- The "TICKETS" sign floats on a bare wall with no booth.
- No signs of use: no posters, people, debris or props.
- One floor and one wall material throughout; lighting is flat.
