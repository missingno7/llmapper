# Gravesend Station: use critique (round 1)

All sizes assume PU = 1,024, since four columns 2,560 apart only fit a hall at least 8,704 wide. 1,024 units = 1.7 m.

## 1. Passenger
- **Street (view_04):** an unmarked dark slot 2,048 wide (3.4 m) between two houses. It has no name, lamp, canopy or forecourt, so nothing tells me it is a station. It could be an alley.
- **Passage and ramp (view_02):** the passage is 5.1 x 6.8 m and nearly 4 bodies tall, then a ramp rises 2.1 m over 6.8 m. It is a gloomy corridor and offers nothing to read on the way in.
- **Ticket:** COUNTER is 512 x 4,096 (0.85 x 6.8 m) and 0.85 m high. It stands only 256 (0.42 m) off the west wall, which is narrower than a body (384), so no clerk can stand behind it. There is no window, grille or office. In view_01 the TICKETS sign is a tiny smudge over what looks like a low bench.
- **Wait:** the hall is 10,240 x 6,144 (17 x 10 m), with no bench, waiting room or stove. It is empty floor. The clock hangs on the south wall, behind me as I enter.
- **Platform and train:** there is one platform (3.4 x 27 m) with no number, lamp or name board. The name GRAVESEND is on the cutting's far wall at 2.6 bodies, behind a car 3.4 bodies tall, so it can never be seen.
- **Board:** no. The car is a solid block 21 m long, **6.8 m wide** (a real carriage is about 2.8 m) and about 6 m over the rail. It has no doors, windows, wheels or ends. In views 03, 05 and 06 it reads as a green-striped wall.
- **Destination:** nothing says where the line goes. The tunnel is unreachable, because the platform edge blocks.

## 2. Missing, in order of value
1. A frontage on the street: a named canopy, lamps and a small forecourt.
2. A booking office with a window or grille and room for a clerk behind it.
3. A train that reads as one: a locomotive at the head, carriage doors and windows at body height, and a guard's van.
4. Benches and a waiting room off the hall.
5. Luggage and parcels: trolleys, trunks and crates, which also serve as cover.
6. Platform lamps, a visible name board and a timetable board.

## 3. Sizes and space
- **Car:** 6.8 m wide and about 6 m tall. It is a building, not a coach.
- **Buffer:** 4,096 across (6.8 m), which is a wall rather than a buffer stop. It also sits at the far end of a track nobody can reach.
- **Counter:** a 0.42 m gap behind it, with no room for a person.
- **Hall:** 170 m² with nothing in it.
- **Platform ends:** both are dead stubs open to sky, 3.4 m and 6.8 m long, with nothing at either one.
- **view_01:** an unexplained black void beside the columns.

## 4. Player
There is nothing to do. The plan admits that nothing moves, there are no enemies or items, there is no switch, and there is no way onward. The station is a cul-de-sac. Three cheap fixes:
1. **Enter the car:** cut one door into the livery side, opening onto a 2.8 m corridor (E1M3's lounge car). Put a key or a weapon inside and a cultist ambush that triggers when you take it.
2. **Staff door behind a real booking window:** the counter becomes a grille. The office behind it is reached around through a side door and holds ammo and the switch.
3. **A way onward:** add steps down at the west platform end and a gate across the tunnel that the office switch opens. Zombies come out of the dark bore. The tunnel leads to the quay or the pump works.

## 5. This town?
It is generic. Every texture is borrowed and nothing says harbour, church or theatre. To tie it to Gravesend:
- fish crates and nets on the platform;
- a coal wagon for the pump works;
- a theatre playbill among the posters;
- the church spire over the cutting wall;
- a sign reading "TO THE QUAY" at the tunnel mouth, making this a harbour branch terminus (which is also what the buffer stop implies).

## 6. Score
**2 / 5.** The bones are right: a climb, a hall, columns and a canopy. As a station, though, it is mute and unusable, and the train is a wall.

## 7. Blocking defects
- The GRAVESEND name hangs behind the 3.4-body car, so no player can see it.
- The street entrance is an unmarked dark slot, with no sign, lamp or canopy.
- The car is 6.8 m wide and about 6 m tall, with no doors, windows, wheels or ends, so it reads as a striped wall.
- The ticket counter is 0.42 m off the wall, so no clerk fits behind it, and it has no window or grille.
- There is no way onward: the platform edge blocks, the track and tunnel are unreachable, and both platform ends are dead.
- There is nothing to do: no enemy, item, switch or moving part.
- The 17 x 10 m hall is empty: no bench, no waiting room.
