# Doors and mechanisms

## What I want to build

Give Gravesend doors and machines that behave like fixtures in their own rooms. A loading gate parts along a track, a fire door swings on hinges, and a wastewater lift carries the player between landings. Reserve the more involved switch logic for a secret the player can work out from clues in the room.

The built city already has type-600 rising and sinking leaves, a keyed type-600 door, a lever-operated roll-up tunnel gate, a type-614 stage curtain that drives the stage light, breakable covers and wired breaches, a switchable conveyor, cycling presses, and a damage sector. The saved map has 31 type-600 sectors, one type-614 sector and one type-618 sector. It has no type-602 sprite-only motion, type-612 path motion, or type-613/615/617 rotation. The city tree contains a turnstile integration helper, though the saved map has no rotor. Sources are the built map, `status.py --wires`, [city status](../../../../projects/blood-city/reports/STATUS.md), and [city wiring](../../../../projects/blood-city/level/wires.py).

## Where Blood built it

Enumerated and parsed 171 maps with `bloodmap.corpus.list_corpus_maps(population="mechanism-tutorial")` and `read_map` (38 top-level tutorials, 98 in Vanilla, and 35 in Modern). The 49 entries below group repeated lesson variants by what the player can do or see. Plain map names identify tutorial stems, `#` is part of a filename, and records are zero-based (`s` sector, `sp` sprite, `w` wall). The top-level and Vanilla examples supply the main catalogue. Modern examples at the end illustrate extension-dependent alternatives. The [curriculum](../../design/mechanism-curriculum-v1.json) has additional motion and marker measurements.

**Authoring support.** **Helper** means the repository already offers an authoring entry point for the central mechanism. **Native** means the map format and authoring primitives can express it by composing sectors, walls, markers, sprites and named wires. These entries identify any higher-level constructor or verification missing. **Modern** requires the engine's extension dialect and verification of any additional fields or runtime behavior. Static chains describe connectivity, with state, locks and timing checked separately. Player actions and feedback below are readings of records and engine behavior unless an endpoint was rendered.

## Sizes

- The normal double solid slider in `DOOR-SLIDINGD` s3 uses a 960-unit marker difference (2.5 player widths) and travels in 1.5 seconds per direction. Its closed panel sits in a sector with 26,624 units of headroom, about 1.57 standing heights.
- The paired swing in `DOOR-SWINGINGD` s12/s30 uses opposite 90° swings and 1.5 seconds per direction. The continuous rotor in `DOOR-ROTATING` s4 describes four full revolutions in its axis angle. Measure its opening at intermediate angles.
- In `MACHINERY-LIFT` s2/s14, the lift rises 32,768 units, or 1.93 standing heights. The low stop has 65,536 units of headroom and the high stop has 32,768. The city dimensions should come from its own landings and shaft.
- `DOOR-COMBIDOORS` retracts the slide by 1,408 units in 5.5 seconds and then takes 6 seconds to swing the leaves. The relay order is the transferable behavior. Select the city stroke times against the real travel and what can be seen from the control.
- Counts and ratios use `PLAYER_PROFILES["blood"]`: 384 units across and 16,960 units high. Keep that height through the intended crossing, plus room at the jamb to operate Use and wait for a safe position.
