# Study: machine-hall

Written 2026-11-25 for the pump station's machine hall, which had been built without one.
Seeds through `tools.study` (E3M6:413!, E3M6:414!, E3M5:9!, DWE2M10:760!) and a gallery sheet of
E3M5:9, E3M5:14, E3M6:414, E3M6:318, E1M8:43, E1M8:245, DWE1M5:9, DWE2M10:760, DWE2M2:679, E6M3:150.
I looked at the frames myself; no blind reader has.

## What I want to build

A hall of machines entered over its floor, where the key is seen before it is reached and the lever
that makes it safe is the second thing the eye finds.

## Where Blood built it

- **E3M6 s413**, the tank hall: 16 by 32 m and 14 m high, its drums ribbed 486/488 on riveted plinths, a
  console bank (s414 to 416: 2462 under a 2288 strip) low on one side. The floor is the LOWER of two levels: a
  walk half a hall up runs between the drums on girder bridges (191), and the moon key stands on it
  between two gunmen, seen from the floor over the walk's edge.
- **E3M5 s9, s44 to s52**: the crushers' line: hazard-striped jambs round everything that shuts, a light in the
  thing itself (s14's round vent, 501) and dark round it.
- **DWE2M10 s760**: a walk above a works floor with an iron rail along its edge (sprite 104, cstat 401,
  repeat 32, every 512), hazard posts at its ends, a strip light down its middle and a WARNING board.
- **E6M3 s150**: the hazard posts again, standing alone in a dark bay.

## What makes it read as that

- **Two levels.** Every one of the halls is looked into from a walk; none is entered on its floor with a machine
  a body from the door, as ours was.
- **The light belongs to one thing.** In E3M6 and E3M5 the hall is near black and the consoles, the vent or
  the strip is the only bright place; the eye goes to it. The hall's own roof is never lit.
- **The rail** says "over", from both sides.
- **Few machines, big.** E3M6's hall holds six drums and a console bank, in 500 square metres.

## Sizes

E3M6 s413: 16.1 x 31.7 m, 13.9 m high; its walk 32,768 over the floor. DWE2M10's walk is 0.9 m wide.
Ours is 13 x 18 m and 10 m; its deck 16,384 over the floor (one body: the rail and the key are in one
glance, and a fall does not hurt).

## What I will transcribe

The rail (104, 32, every 512, solid and two-sided), the strip light as the underside of a hood over the
thing that matters (E3M6 s416: 2288), the hazard strips either side of the lever (E3M5 s14), the
2462/2463/2477 consoles and the presses already transcribed from E3M5 s45 to 50.

## What I will not do, and why

No girder bridge over the hall: a walkable one is a room over a room, and the hall's floor is under it. No second
drum: it hid the hall. No roof lights: at six bodies they light nothing (the light model's lamp falls to a
twenty-fifth at that distance, and the old ones showed no pool).

## What I still do not know

Whether the belt, met at a walk, carries a player fast enough to be feared: the owner's walk will say.
