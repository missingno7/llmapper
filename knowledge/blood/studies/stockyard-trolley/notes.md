# Study: stockyard-trolley

## What I want to build

A small hand-operated sampling trolley across the blood race at the end of
Market Slip's drain. The west maintenance walk leads to the boarding ledge.
The east berth serves a dry inspection alcove.

## Where Blood built it

- [#2WAYCAR](../doors-and-mechanisms/notes.md), sector 1 and markers 0/1.
  Four redwalls join a rectangular deck to the moving hole in sector 0.
  Type 616 and drag carry occupants. The marker delta is (0, 21,504).
- E1M2:34, the railway. Tile 1129 puts the two rails and sleepers on one
  plane. E1M2 sprites 384–391 put wheelsets on the side of a vehicle.

## What makes it read as that

The tutorial's red floor patch stays one rectangle under a continuous
ceiling. The station frame shows the paired rails converging down a long
bay. In the drain, the rail line leads the eye from the west ledge to a
lit booth across the current. The deck needs open ends to read as a
place to step onto from either shore.

## Sizes

The tutorial's deck is 2,048 by 2,048 units with 30,720 of headroom.
One Blood body is 384 wide and 16,960 high. The smaller sampling deck
can use the same constant ceiling across the chamber and land flush to
the shored landings.

## What I will transcribe

The tutorial's inside-out redwall loop and full-wall translation.
The station's floor track and carried wheel artwork. The drain's tile
and blood palette continue around the track bed.

## What I will not do, and why

The tutorial departs on Enter. The landings here call their own stop and
the deck gets a separate departure button so the rider can board before
it moves. The race keeps its fast current, while the broader sampling
chamber has quiet eddies beside the track.

## What I still do not know

Whether riders find the controls intuitively, time their steps onto the
deck and can recover comfortably after stepping off in transit. The
observer can show the endpoints and the owner can walk the crossing.
